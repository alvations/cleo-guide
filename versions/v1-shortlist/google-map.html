Google Maps rendering

Each day shows a routed Google map. It works with no key using Google's classic embed. For the officially supported embed — sharper, and stable long-term — paste a Google Maps Embed API key below. It is stored only in this browser and never leaves it.

Keyless mode — Google's classic embed.

Why two maps. The Leaflet edition is the primary shortlist: it works offline, needs no key, and never depends on Google. This page is the opposite trade — it hands the routing to Google so you get turn-by-turn in the app you already use. Pick whichever suits the trip.

Degrades honestly. If Google's embed is blocked, each map shows a note and the itinerary still reads in full, with a working Open in Google Maps link on every day and every stop. No content depends on the map loading.

Business details come from Google and change without notice. Hours in the notes were right at time of writing — confirm before a long drive. Map data © Google.

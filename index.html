Silicon Valley

Not one city but the South Bay — Palo Alto, Mountain View, Sunnyvale, Cupertino, Santa Clara and San Jose. Apple Park, the Computer History Museum, Stanford and the Winchester Mystery House; and the Valley's real signature, its immigrant kitchens — arguably the best Taiwanese, Vietnamese and South Indian eating in America, plus the boba it invented. Filter by city or by collection (tech landmarks, museums, parks…), plus cuisines.

150 places, each clearing a ≥2-credible-source bar (Michelin, James Beard, Infatuation, KQED, Metro Silicon Valley, San José Spotlight, The Six Fifty & more) with a fact-checked, source-cited coordinate. Filter by city or collection.

Columbus

Ohio's capital — the Short North arts district, German Village and the Book Loft, the Scioto Mile and Ohio State. And a sleeper-great food town: Jeni's ice cream (born here), the North Market, Columbus-style square-cut pizza, Schmidt's German cream puffs, and one of the deepest Somali tables in America. Same ≥2-credible-source pipeline as our other cities.

Dayton

The birthplace of aviation — the National Museum of the U.S. Air Force (the world's largest, and free), Carillon Historical Park with the Wright Flyer III, and the Wright brothers' own workshops in Wright-Dunbar. Plus the Oregon District's bars, Marion's Dayton-style pizza, Esther Price candy and Bill's Donuts — out to Yellow Springs. Same ≥2-credible-source pipeline as our other cities.

Cologne · Bonn · Düsseldorf

The Rhineland

The lower-Rhine triangle on one map — Cologne (the UNESCO Dom, twelve Romanesque churches and the Kölsch brewhouses), Bonn (Beethoven and the Museumsmeile) and Düsseldorf (the Altstadt “longest bar”, Altbier, the Kö and Little Tokyo). Halve Hahn and Himmel un Ääd to Japanese ramen, with a deep Michelin bench (Ox & Klee, Le Moissonnier, Im Schiffchen).

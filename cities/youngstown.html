How this list was built — read this

Researched by web search (August 2026) and fact-checked against the ranked sources below. Specific facts were confirmed against those sources — the Butler opened in 1919 as the first U.S. museum of American art and is free; Mill Creek Park runs to 4,400+ acres; the Melnick museum keeps a 1952 iron lung; the Ward Beecher Planetarium runs free public shows. Every source is logged in data/sources.json and audited by tools/research.js. It still isn't a full numbered-source transcription like Cleveland, so confirm current hours before a drive and treat anything marked verify as a lead. Direct page fetches are blocked in the build environment, so sourcing came through search results, not full-page reads.

Where to eat

Youngstown's signature is Brier Hill pizza — a pie with bell peppers and grated romano and little or no mozzarella, born in the old Brier Hill steelworkers' neighbourhood and documented by Gastro Obscura. These spots were fact-checked against local food sources; confirm hours before a drive.

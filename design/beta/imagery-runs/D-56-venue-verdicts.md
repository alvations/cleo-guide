# D-56 re-check of the wave-6 "venue" skips (imagery, 2026-10-17)

The test is D-56 (9938de852). A venue stays skipped when a photo of it would be a shopfront, signage, an interior fit-out or an event crowd. A venue is lifted when its building or structure is itself the landmark: heritage-listed (NRHP/NHL or a national/municipal designation), or with its own article as a building. A lifted target gets only an exterior with the architecture as the subject, under the usual rules.

Lifted with photos earlier (39574000): Cincinnati Music Hall, Findlay Market, SF cable cars (NHL), Kennedy Center. Each has an approved LoC exterior.

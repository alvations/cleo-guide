    Downtown & The Flats · SeeChess Collection — Cleveland Public Library MAIN Library<strong>This is the right building.</strong> The 1925 Main Library on Superior Avenue — not a branch, not the Louis Stokes Wing next door. Go in the Main Building, take the marble staircase to the third floor, and pass through the John Griswold White Exhibition Corridor (look for owls in the wrought-iron cases) into Special Collections.<br><br>Inside is the John G. White Chess and Checkers Collection: the largest chess collection in the world. Over 35,000 items going back to the 12th century — one-of-a-kind sets from every continent, rare manuscripts, Bobby Fischer score sheets, Lasker medals, the most comprehensive tournament records anywhere, and two death masks of Gisela Kahn Gresser, nine-time US women's champion. White was a Cleveland attorney who collected from 1870 until his death in 1928, buying up entire European and American chess libraries; the library has kept collecting since. It has been on this floor since the doors opened.
    Must see

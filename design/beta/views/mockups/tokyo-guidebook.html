Chiyoda-ku

Imperial Palace · Marunouchi · Akihabara · Kanda · Jimbōchō

Chiyoda-ku holds 43 places in this guide: 26 sights and 17 places to eat or drink. Its must-sees start with Imperial Palace East Gardens and Yasukuni Shrine. 7 places here belong to the pop-culture trail. The source that names the most of them is Time Out (27).

Top picks 17

  1. 1Imperial Palace East Gardens 皇居東御苑

    Must see1-1 Chiyoda, Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo 100-8111

    The free public garden on the old honmaru of Edo Castle — the castle-keep base, guardhouses and a seasonal garden inside the palace walls.

    Look for: Edo Castle ruins, free admission

    Recommended by WikipediaGO TokyoTime Out

  2. 2Yasukuni Shrine 靖國神社

    Must see3-1-1 Kudankita, Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo 102-8246

    The 1869 Shintō shrine to Japan's war dead — great torii, cherry-tree avenue and the Yūshūkan war museum; politically contested, historically essential.

    Recommended by WikipediaGO TokyoJapan-guide

  3. 3Kanda Myōjin 神田明神

    Must see2-16-2 Sotokanda, Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo 101-0021

    1,300-year-old shrine of Edo's merchants, home of the Kanda Matsuri — and, a block from Akihabara, an anime pilgrimage stop (Love Live!) with IT-blessing omamori.

    Recommended by GO TokyoWikipediaTime Out

  4. 4Tokyo Station Marunouchi Building 東京駅丸の内駅舎

    Must seeTokyo Station, Marunouchi 1-chōme, Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo

    Tatsuno Kingo's 1914 red-brick station front, restored with its twin domes in 2012 — a nationally Important Cultural Property.

    Recommended by GO TokyoJapan-guideWikipedia

  5. 5Akihabara Electric Town 秋葉原電気街

    Must seeSotokanda 1-chome, Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo

    Tokyo's electronics-and-otaku quarter — multi-storey arcades, figure shops, retro-game dens and maid cafés around Chūō-dōri.

    Recommended by GO TokyoJapan-guideTime Out

  6. 6Kōkyo Gaien & Nijūbashi 皇居外苑・二重橋

    Must seeKōkyo Gaien, Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo (in front of the Imperial Palace)

    The Imperial Palace's outer garden — a gravel plaza of 2,000 sculpted black pines leading to the classic view of the Nijūbashi bridges and Fushimi turret over the moat.

    Look for: Nijūbashi view, black pines

    Recommended by WikipediaGO TokyoTime Out

  7. 7Isegen いせ源

    Must see1-11-1 Kanda-Sudachō, Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo 101-0041

    Tokyo's only anko-nabe specialist, cooking monkfish hotpot since 1830 in a 1930 wooden house — no reservations, no cards, open in the cold season.

    Recommended by Savor JapanThe Japan TimesPaolo fromTOKYO

  8. 8Virtù, Four Seasons Hotel Tokyo at Otemachi

    Must seeFour Seasons Hotel Tokyo at Otemachi, 1-2-1 Otemachi, Chiyoda City, Tokyo 100-0004 (hotel address per Google Hotels; bar floor not confirmed)

    Art-deco cocktail bar at the Four Seasons Otemachi; the Virtù Martini uses Japanese gin and vodka with hinoki bitters. Asia's 50 Best Bars 2026 No 26 and World's 50 Best Bars 2025.

    Recommended by Time OutThe World's 50 Best Bars / Asia's Official Site

  9. 9Chidorigafuchi 千鳥ヶ淵緑道

    Must seeChidorigafuchi Ryokudō, Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo

    The 700 m promenade above Edo Castle's moat under some 230 cherry trees — Tokyo's most beloved hanami walk, with rowboats on the water below and night illuminations in season.

    Look for: moat-side hanami, rowboats

    Recommended by GO TokyoTime Out

  10. 10Mandarake Complex Akihabara まんだらけコンプレックス

    Must see3-11-12 Sotokanda, Chiyoda City, Tokyo

    Eight-storey flagship of the second-hand otaku chain, each floor sorted by product type — vintage 'Japanese Pop Antique' figures, magical-girl toys and cosplay, manga, dōjinshi and more (Time Out, Tokyo Cheapo).

    Look for: ★ Anime & pop culture — Anime & manga collectibles — the biggest single Mandarake, Akihabara's deep-cut used-figure and dōjinshi tower · Otaku megastore

    Recommended by Time OutTokyo Cheapo

  11. 11Kanda Yabu Soba かんだやぶそば

    Must see2-10 Kanda-Awajichō, Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo 101-0063

    The patriarch of Tokyo's Yabu soba lineage (1880) — rebuilt as a one-storey house after the 2013 fire; staff still sing orders to the kitchen.

    Recommended by Time OutThe Japan Times

  12. 12Kanda Matsuya 神田まつや

    Must see1-13 Kanda-Sudachō, Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo 101-0041

    Soba house since 1884 in a Taishō-era wooden building a minute from Awajichō Station — more down-to-earth and cheaper than Yabu, just as beloved.

    Recommended by Savor JapanThe Japan Times

  13. 13RyuGin, Tokyo Midtown Hibiya 日本料理 龍吟

    Must see7F, Tokyo Midtown Hibiya, 1-1-2 Yūrakuchō, Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo 100-0006

    Seiji Yamamoto's celebrated Japanese restaurant, moved from Roppongi to the 7th floor of Tokyo Midtown Hibiya — three MICHELIN stars in the 2026 guide.

    Recommended by MichelinWikipedia

  14. 14Botan, Kanda-Sudachō ぼたん

    Must see1-15 Kanda-Sudachō, Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo

    Founded in the last years of the 19th century and rebuilt after the 1923 earthquake in the two-storey timber house it still occupies: one dish only, chicken sukiyaki bubbling in a cast-iron pot over glowing charcoal.

    Recommended by Time OutThe Japan Times

  15. 15Sézanne

    Must see7F Four Seasons Hotel Tokyo at Marunouchi, 1-11-1 Marunouchi, Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo 100-6277

  16. 16Kioicho Fukudaya 紀尾井町 福田家

    Recommended by Michelin

    Worth the detour2-3 Kitanomaru-koen, Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo 102-8321

    Worth the detourHibiya-kōen, Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo

    Look for: Western-style park, open-air concerts

    Recommended by WikipediaGO TokyoTime Out

  3. 20National Diet Building 国会議事堂

    Worth the detour1-7-1 Nagatachō, Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo 100-0014

    Look for: free weekday tours

    Worth the detour2-6-2 Marunouchi, Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo 100-0005

    Look for: Josiah Conder red brick

  5. 25National Museum of Modern Art, Tokyo (MOMAT)

    Worth the detour3-1 Kitanomaru-koen, Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo

    Recommended by WikipediaTime Out

  6. 26Jimbōchō Book Town 神保町古書店街

    Worth the detourKanda-Jimbōchō 1–2-chome (Yasukuni-dōri × Hakusan-dōri), Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo

    Recommended by WikipediaTime Out

  7. 27Hie Shrine 日枝神社

    Worth the detour2-10-5 Nagatachō, Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo

    Recommended by WikipediaTime Out

  8. 28Holy Resurrection Cathedral — Nikolai-dō ニコライ堂

    Worth the detourKanda-Surugadai, Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo

    Look for: Byzantine dome

  9. 29Tokyo International Forum 東京国際フォーラム

    Worth the detour3-5-1 Marunouchi, Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo 100-0005

    Look for: glass hall, antique market

    Recommended by WikipediaGO Tokyo

  10. 30Kitanomaru Park 北の丸公園

    Worth the detour1-1 Kitanomaru-kōen, Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo 102-0091

    Wooded former Edo Castle enclosure north of the palace — the Budōkan, the Science Museum and MOMAT among cherry trees by the Chidorigafuchi moat.

    Look for: cherry trees by the moat

    Recommended by WikipediaTime Out

  11. 32Tokyo Daijingū 東京大神宮

    Worth the detour2-4-1 Fujimi, Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo 102-0071

    The Tokyo branch of Ise, where the first Shintō wedding was held in 1901 — now the city's favourite shrine for praying for love, with matchmaking omikuji.

    Look for: love and marriage shrine

    Recommended by WikipediaGO Tokyo

  12. 41Seikadō Bunko Art Museum 静嘉堂文庫美術館

    Deep cutMeiji Seimei-kan, Marunouchi, Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo

  13. 42Marunouchi Building 丸の内ビルディング

  14. 43National Shōwa Memorial Museum — Shōwa-kan 昭和館

    Deep cut1-6-1 Kudan-minami, Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo 102-0074

Chūō-ku

Chūō-ku holds 49 places in this guide: 13 sights and 36 places to eat or drink. Its must-sees start with Tsukiji Hongan-ji and Pokémon Café Tokyo, Nihonbashi. 1 place here belong to the pop-culture trail. The source that names the most of them is Michelin (20).

  1. 1Tsukiji Hongan-ji 築地本願寺

    Must seeTsukiji 3-chōme, Chūō-ku, Tokyo

    Startling 1934 Indian-style Buddhist temple by Itō Chūta, with a pipe organ inside and a breakfast café — at the gate of the Tsukiji outer market.

    Look for: Indo-Islamic facade, pipe organ

    Recommended by WikipediaGO TokyoTime Out

  2. 2Pokémon Café Tokyo, Nihonbashi ポケモンカフェ 日本橋

    The world's first Pokémon Café (2018), beside Pokémon Center Tokyo DX; reopened 17 June 2026 after renovation with Pikachu curry, Eevee pancakes, the new Pika Pika Parfait and a live Chef Pikachu show — the Gengar smoothie stays.

    Look for: ★ Anime & pop culture — Pokémon — the franchise's original themed café, reservation-only, with a live Pikachu show

Kantō day trips

Yokohama · Kamakura · Hakone · Nikkō · Kawagoe · Fuji Five Lakes

Kantō day trips holds 39 places in this guide: 35 sights and 4 places to eat or drink. Its must-sees start with Great Buddha of Kamakura — Kōtoku-in and Tsurugaoka Hachimangū. 3 places here belong to the pop-culture trail. The source that names the most of them is Wikipedia (36).

Top picks 14

  1. 1Great Buddha of Kamakura — Kōtoku-in 鎌倉大仏

    Must see4-2-28 Hase, Kamakura, Kanagawa 248-0016

    The 13.35 m bronze Amida Buddha (1252) sitting in the open air since a 15th-century tsunami washed its hall away — you can step inside the hollow statue.

    Look for: open-air bronze Buddha

    Recommended by WikipediaJapan-guide

  2. 2Tsurugaoka Hachimangū 鶴岡八幡宮

    Look for: Dankazura approach

    Recommended by WikipediaJapan-guide

  3. 3Nikkō Tōshō-gū 日光東照宮

    Must see2301 Sannai, Nikkō, Tochigi 321-1431

    Tokugawa Ieyasu's lavishly carved mausoleum shrine in cedar forest — the Yōmeimon gate, the three wise monkeys and the sleeping cat; part of the UNESCO Shrines and Temples of Nikkō.

    Look for: Yōmeimon, three wise monkeys

    Recommended by WikipediaUNESCO

  4. 4Hakone Shrine 箱根神社

    Must see80-1 Motohakone, Hakone, Kanagawa 250-0522

    Look for: sculpture park, Picasso pavilion

Le Corbusier's only building in East Asia — part of the UNESCO Le Corbusier serial site — housing the Matsukata collection of Rodin, Monet and Old Masters; Rodin's Gates of Hell stand in the forecourt.

Postwar black-market street under the Yamanote tracks between Ueno and Okachimachi — fishmongers shouting year-end deals, dried goods, spices, sneakers and standing izakaya.

Sensō-ji's 'Thunder Gate' with its 700 kg red paper lantern and the wind and thunder gods — the start of Nakamise-dōri and Tokyo's most photographed gateway.

Serving loach simmered in a shallow iron pan since 1801 — the Edo working-man's unagi — at low tables on a tatami floor by the Sumida.

10笹乃雪
Must see· Taitō-ku · Tofu

Tokyo's most famous tofu restaurant, founded in the Edo period by a tofu-maker brought from Kyoto by Kan'ei-ji's imperial abbot — silken tofu in sweet ankake sauce, by Uguisudani Station.

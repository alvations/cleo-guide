Asakusa Shrine浅草神社

78 m away

The 1649 Gongen-style shrine beside Sensō-ji honouring the temple's three founders, home of the riotous Sanja Matsuri each May.

Asakusa Kagetsudō浅草花月堂

143 m away

Asakusa fixture since 1945 that sells essentially one thing — a crisp-topped jumbo melonpan best eaten warm, often sold out by 4pm.

Hanayashiki浅草花やしき

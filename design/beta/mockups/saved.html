Tokyo's oldest temple (founded 645) — through the Kaminarimon thunder gate and the Nakamise-dōri stall street to the main hall and five-storey pagoda.

2三鷹の森ジブリ
Must see· Tama area

Ghibli Museum, Mitaka三鷹の森ジブリ美術館

Hayao Miyazaki's own whimsical museum on the edge of Inokashira Park — animation workshops, a Catbus room, a rooftop Laputa robot and short films shown only here. Advance tickets only.

Recommended byWikipediaGo Tokyo

Must see· Chūō-ku · Ramen

A tiny Ginza counter where a French-trained chef builds a clear, consommé-like shōyu chūka soba — Tokyo-style ramen at its most refined.

6うさぎや
Must see· Taitō-ku · Wagashi

Usagiyaうさぎや

Century-old Ueno wagashi shop widely cited as one of Tokyo's three great dorayaki makers; the Japan Times profiled it as one of the two shops claiming to have created the dorayaki. Its dorayaki uses aged azuki paste in a honey-infused batter.

Recommended byTime OutThe Japan Times+1
7谷中銀座商店街
Must see· Taitō-ku · Markets

Yanaka Ginza Shōtengai谷中銀座商店街

A 60-shop old-Tokyo shopping street between Nippori and Sendagi, best grazed: beef menchi katsu from Niku no Suzuki (since 1980), cat-shaped taiyaki, manjū and matcha, all below the 'sunset steps'.

Recommended byTime OutTokyo Cheapo+1

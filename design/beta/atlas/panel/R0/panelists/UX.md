### Top three fixes
1. **Make pins identify places: names at zoom, unique labels, quieter clusters.** Show the place name next to a pin wherever it fits (at least for must-sees and the selected or hovered area). Make on-map labels unique within the viewport, using one global rank or a kind glyph instead of per-area ranks. Restyle clusters as light, smaller discs that stay out of the way. Give food a glyph, not just a sand fill. *Acceptance:* at Tokyo desktop default+2 zoom, `namedLabels ≥ 10`, no duplicate label text among visible pins, and cluster count ≤ 15 with no cluster over 99 on the phone first screen. A user test identifies sight vs food from a pin alone in 9 of 10 cases.
2. **Keep your place: restore the view after detail, and put the view in the URL.** Closing the detail (Escape, ×, Back) returns the map to `viewBefore` (centre within 1 px, same zoom) and the list to its prior scroll, with no page scroll. `#view=atlas` carries `@lat,lng,z`. Deep links offset the centre so the pin lands in the visible map beside the panel. *Acceptance:* `d-roundtrip.viewKept` and `m-roundtrip.viewKept` are true on all 4 pages; `mapViewInUrl=true` (T5 reopen within 1 px and the same z); `pinUnderSheet=false` on every deep link; focus returns to the card on all 4 pages.
3. **Make the list answer "what's best near here".** Once the user locates or zooms past ~z14, rank the list and carousel by distance plus tier, flattening the area grouping. Show walking distance on cards. Make the phone carousel always start with a place inside the current viewport. Cut the counters to one line ("23 here · Show all 225"). *Acceptance:* rerunning T3 (Shibuya 35.6595, 139.7005), the first desktop card and the first phone carousel card are both within 1.2 km and include Nonbei Yokochō or The Bellwood in the top 3. Every card shows a distance. The first carousel card's coordinate is inside the map bounds in 100% of probe states. At most 2 count strings are visible above the first card.

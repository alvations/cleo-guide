# Competitive research A: tours & experiences platforms, OTAs, hotel loyalty

Research date: 2026-10-10. Method: WebSearch only (~45 queries). Every claim carries a URL that appeared in search results. Third-party affiliate directories often disagree; where they do, the conflict is noted. "Not found" means the searches did not surface it.

---

## Market facts

- **Market size:** Arival and Phocuswright put global travel experiences at **$271B in 2025**, forecast to reach **$342B by 2029**, an 8% nominal CAGR for 2023-2029 against about 5% for travel overall. https://www.traveldailynews.com/statistics-trends/global-experiences-market-projected-to-reach-342bn-by-2029/ ; https://arival.travel/research/the-outlook-for-travel-experiences-2019-2025/
- **Online share:** only **33% of experiences gross bookings were made online in 2025**, against 64% across travel overall. The online share is projected to reach **42% by 2029**. https://www.traveldailynews.com/statistics-trends/global-experiences-market-projected-to-reach-342bn-by-2029/
- **Mobile and last-minute booking:** Arival finds mobile tour bookers spend almost 50% more than desktop bookers and average 2.9 tours per trip. https://v2.arival.travel/?p=50921
- Arival/Phocuswright analysis shows many more bookings made less than 2 days ahead, and a drop in walk-up bookings. https://www.phocuswire.com/Why-mobile-is-vital-for-in-destination-bookings
- Trekksoft data cited by Viator puts 38% of bookings on the day of the activity or up to two days before. Skift data cited by Viator says 35% of travelers have booked a tour on mobile while already in the destination. https://operatorresources.viator.com/in-destination-bookers-cutoff-time-hours-in-advance/
- A precise mobile share of tour bookings was not found.

## 1. Airbnb Experiences (and Services, new app)

- **Positioning:** On 13 May 2025 Airbnb relaunched Experiences alongside a new **Services** category (chefs, trainers and similar, in 260 cities) and a redesigned app. Experiences launched in about 650 cities. The relaunch ended a two-year pause on accepting new experiences. https://www.altexsoft.com/travel-industry-news/airbnb-relaunches-experiences-and-adds-services-will-travelers-care-this-time/ ; https://arival.travel/?p=187663 ; https://hospitalitydesign.com/news/airbnb-services-experiences-new-app/179155
- **Audience:** Anyone using the app, not only people who have booked a stay. https://www.phocuswire.com/airbnb-summer-release-2025-experiences-services-short-term-rentals
- **Business model:** a fee of about **20% of the host's price for Experiences** and about **15% for Services**. Lodging moved to a host-only fee of about 15.5% from October 2025. https://boostly.co.uk/how-much-does-airbnb-charge-hosts/ ; https://hospitable.com/airbnb-experiences
- Guests see the full price with no added fee. https://hospitable.com/airbnb-experiences
- Affiliate program: not found.
- **Core features:**
  - Categories: History & Culture, Food & Drink, Nature & Outdoors, Art & Design, Fitness & Wellness.
  - "Landmarks" tours of major attractions.
  - **Airbnb Originals**, hosted by personalities such as Sabrina Carpenter and Patrick Mahomes.
  - Sources: https://www.xola.com/articles/what-are-airbnb-experiences/ ; https://arival.travel/?p=187663
  - The new host app has a calendar, simpler applications and group messaging. https://summitstocks.substack.com/p/airbnbs-2025-summer-release
- **Trust model:** Airbnb vets every Experience before it goes live, and guests leave reviews. https://www.rentalscaleup.com/airbnb-experiences-airbnb-services-2025/
- Services hosts include chefs from Michelin-starred restaurants. https://www.phocuswire.com/airbnb-summer-release-2025-experiences-services-short-term-rentals
- During the pause Airbnb removed about 5,000 listings that had low bookings or fell below its standards. https://shorttermrentalz.com/news/airbnb-experiences-listings-applications-reopen/
- **Personalisation and distribution:** cross-sold inside the lodging app to Airbnb's existing users. No specific personalisation detail found.
- **Strategic shift (August 2026):**
  - Airbnb and Tripadvisor Group (Viator) announced a partnership to sell Tripadvisor/Viator experiences inside Airbnb, launching later in 2026. https://tripadvisor.gcs-web.com/node/22141 ; https://www.phocuswire.com/news/distribution/airbnb-partners-tripadvisor-scale-experiences
  - Skift calls it a move away from the "build-your-own" strategy: the relaunch had about 3,000 experiences in roughly 100 cities, and Airbnb then narrowed its focus to Paris. https://skift.com/2026/08/11/airbnb-partners-with-tripadvisor-experiences-drops-build-your-own-strategy/
  - Open questions: whether Viator will fulfil every listing, how reviews will appear, and whether listings will be labelled. https://deeparrival.com/news/airbnb-tripadvisor-tours-partnership/
- **Weaknesses:**
  - The business is still small. On the Q2 2026 call Chesky said Airbnb added 1,000 experiences and supply grew nearly 80%. https://www.phocuswire.com/news/distribution/airbnb-partners-tripadvisor-scale-experiences
  - Hosts blamed past problems on oversaturation and falling quality. https://arival.travel/?p=154359
  - Analysts raised concerns about inconsistent quality and dilution of the core product. https://summitstocks.substack.com/p/airbnbs-2025-summer-release
  - The 20% fee is higher than the lodging fee. https://boostly.co.uk/how-much-does-airbnb-charge-hosts/
- **Scale:** about 3,000 experiences at relaunch, plus about 1,000 added (see the Skift and PhocusWire links above). No revenue figure for Experiences alone was found.
- For company context, Q2 2026 total revenue was $3.6B, up 17%. https://transcripts.platformaeronaut.com/transcripts/ABNB-1Q26-transcript

## 2. GetYourGuide

- **Positioning and audience:** a global, Berlin-based experiences marketplace. It is the largest independent player in the category and reached profitability on an adjusted EBITDA basis in 2025. https://skift.com/2025/10/21/getyourguide-profitable-travel-experiences/
- **Business model:** commission from operators.
  - Arival reported a commission increase announced at short notice in peak season; one Australian operator called 29% "really high". https://arival.travel/article/getyourguide-commission-increasing-for-some-operators
  - A vendor page claims 20-35%. This is unverified. https://rapidevelopers.com/clone/getyourguide
- **Affiliate program:** usually **8%** with a 30-day cookie, according to third-party directories. Some list 7% for the US and for Content Partnerships. Commission is reversed on cancellations. https://uppromote.com/affiliate-directory/getyourguide/ ; https://affilimate.io/programs/getyourguide-affiliate-program/ ; https://getlasso.co/affiliate/get-your-guide/
- **Core features:**
  - "Originals" (GetYourGuide's own products).
  - Creator videos on product pages, made with a community of 10,000 creators (2024).
  - An AI Content Creator that helps suppliers write listings. https://www.phocuswire.com/getyourguide-product-launch-ai-influencer-video
  - Fall 2025: AI review management with an "AI Reply Optimizer", a redesigned listing page, AI filters. https://www.phocuswire.com/getyourguide-experiences-ai-features-fall-product-release
  - Spring 2026: AI review summaries drawn from verified guests, more personalised search. https://skift.com/2026/04/22/getyourguide-ai-updates/
  - A ChatGPT app is mentioned only in a secondary summary page and was not verified. https://simplify.jobs/c/GetYourGuide
- **Trust model:** reviews from verified bookers, with AI summaries (Skift link above). Trustpilot shows about 4/5 from 50k+ reviews. https://au.trustpilot.com/review/www.getyourguide.com?page=10
- **Content freshness:** AI tools for supplier listings and review replies (links above).
- **Personalisation:** personalised search from 2026 (Skift link above).
- **Distribution:** SEO, the affiliate program, and creator video (links above).
- **Weaknesses:**
  - In 2020 the CEO admitted GetYourGuide had favoured its own Originals in search ranking. https://skift.com/2020/11/04/ceo-of-getyourguide-concedes-it-had-its-own-google-like-bias-issue/
  - Operator backlash over commission increases (Arival link above).
  - Travelers complain about last-minute operator cancellations, guides who don't show up and slow refunds. https://ghostnet.vcu.edu/is-get-your-guide-legit-reviews-safety-and-red-flags
- **Scale:**
  - **Revenue over €1B in 2025, 33M experiences booked.** https://www.streetinsider.com/Press+Releases/GetYourGuide+Appoints+Rob+Rekrutiak+as+Chief+Product+Officer/26087170.html
  - Q3 2025 GMV up 30%, with 10M experiences booked in the quarter. https://www.venturelab.swiss/GetYourGuide-reaches-profitability-and-nears-1-billion-in-revenue
  - About 50,000 supply partners and 200,000 experiences (summarised from the release).
  - An unverified LinkedIn claim says 18M+ bookings in H1 2026, with GMV up 27%. https://de.linkedin.com/in/manishpatel79

## 3. Viator / Tripadvisor

- **Positioning:** Tripadvisor is pivoting from hotel metasearch to "a leading experiences marketplace". Viator is its growth engine. It is selling TheFork to American Express. https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/tripadvisor-q1-earnings-call-highlights
- **Audience:** global leisure travelers, drawn largely from Tripadvisor's review audience.
- **Business model:** operators pay a commission, commonly cited at 20-30%. "Accelerate" (2025) lets operators pay above the standard rate for better placement. https://www.thetraveler.org/should-you-book-tours-through-viator-or-directly/
- **Affiliate program:** Viator's official Awin profiles show **4% default commission, 30-day cookie**, paid after travel. Aggregators say 8%. https://ui.awin.com/merchant-profile/11018 ; https://uppromote.com/affiliate-directory/viator/
- **Core features and AI:**
  - Tripadvisor's AI itinerary builder launched in 2023 (US beta). https://kiplinger.com/personal-finance/tripadvisor-launches-ai-powered-travel-itinerary-generator
  - A Tripadvisor app inside ChatGPT for hotel discovery, open to logged-in US users. https://www.tripadvisor.co.uk/ChatGPT
  - Tripadvisor and Viator apps launched within Claude.
  - AI pre-booking chat in the Viator app.
  - Partnerships with OpenAI, Perplexity, Microsoft, Amazon and Anthropic.
  - Source for the last three points: https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/tripadvisor-q1-earnings-call-highlights
- **Trust model:** about 1 billion user reviews (Nasdaq link above).
  - The 2025 Transparency Report covers 2024: **2.7M fraudulent reviews stopped (about 8% of 31.1M submitted)**, 54% of them from owner self-promotion, and 214k AI-generated reviews removed. 4.9% of reviews went to human moderators. https://www.hospitalitynet.org/news/4126305/tripadvisors-2025-transparency-report-reveals-strong-review-submissions-and-improved-fraud-detection ; https://www.nbcsandiego.com/news/business/money-report/heres-how-many-fake-reviews-tripadvisor-found-on-its-website-in-2024/3833586/?os=vb
- **Content freshness:** a constant flow of user-generated reviews plus automated moderation (links above).
- **Distribution:**
  - SEO from the Tripadvisor domain.
  - Wholesale distribution: Booking.com and, from late 2026, Airbnb. https://skift.com/2025/09/29/booking-com-to-add-150000-attractions-with-fareharbor-tie-in/ ; https://tripadvisor.gcs-web.com/node/22141
- **Weaknesses:**
  - Fake-review volume doubled since 2022 (NBC link above).
  - BBB complaints about last-minute cancellations without refunds, and "free cancellation" claims that don't match the 7-day "strict" policies on some listings. https://truthinadvertising.org/articles/viators-free-cancellation-claims/
  - Operators confused by changing commission rules (The Traveler link above).
  - The group's revenue is falling: Q1 2026 revenue was down 4%, and activist investor Starboard is pressuring the company. https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/tripadvisor-q1-earnings-call-highlights
  - Q2 2026 Experiences segment grew only 3%. https://hospitality-on.com/en/events/tripadvisor-hands-part-its-experiences-catalog-airbnb
- **Scale:**
  - FY2025 Experiences revenue **$924M (+10%)**, **22.9M bookings (+16%)**.
  - Experiences are about 50% of group revenue and 30% of group profit. Group revenue was $1.891B.
  - Source: https://www.altexsoft.com/travel-industry-news/tripadvisor-experiences-drive-30-of-2025-profit/ ; https://www.phocuswire.com/tripadvisor-q4-2025-earnings
  - Catalogue of more than 425,000 experiences. https://tripadvisor.gcs-web.com/node/22141

## 7. Booking.com (Booking Holdings)

- **Positioning:** the largest OTA, building a "connected trip" by cross-selling attractions.
  - In September 2025 it announced it would add **150,000 attractions via FareHarbor**, a company it owns. Existing attractions partners are Musement, Viator and Klook, covering 5,000+ cities in 115 countries.
  - It uses AI to group similar experiences and support conversational search, tied to Genius.
  - Sources: https://skift.com/2025/09/29/booking-com-to-add-150000-attractions-with-fareharbor-tie-in/ ; https://www.travolution.com/news/travel-sectors/tours-and-activities/booking.com-integrates-with-fareharbor/
- **Loyalty (Genius):**
  - Level 1 (any signed-in account): 10% off eligible properties.
  - Level 2 (5 stays in 2 years): up to 15% off.
  - Level 3 (15 stays in 2 years): up to 20% off.
  - Genius rewards also apply to attractions.
  - Sources: https://pointhacks.com.au/guides/loyalty-programs/booking-com-genius ; https://www.travolution.com/news/travel-sectors/tours-and-activities/booking.com-integrates-with-fareharbor/
- **AI:**
  - AI Trip Planner built on the ChatGPT API, US beta from June 2023. https://www.bookingholdings.com/press-releases/booking-com-launches-new-ai-trip-planner-to-enhance-travel-planning-experience/
  - Smart Filter, Property Q&A and Review Summaries (October 2024). https://www.bookingholdings.com/?p=11396
  - A Booking.com ChatGPT app: not found.
- **Affiliate program:** since June 2025 runs via CJ, with **4% on completed attractions**. The older direct program paid 25% of Booking.com's own commission. https://www.makeinfluence.com/en/academy/booking-coms-affiliate-program-how-travel-creators-earn-commission ; https://www.travelpayouts.com/en/offers/bookingcom-affiliate-program
- **Trust model:** reviews from verified stays.
- **Weaknesses:**
  - Which? found it could list a fake property in under 15 minutes with no ID check.
  - Scam warnings were buried in reviews.
  - Sources: https://www.which.co.uk/policy-and-insight/article/lack-of-proper-checks-is-leaving-booking.com-wide-open-to-scammers-which-warns-arv5l3b8k7t6 ; https://www.which.co.uk/news/article/the-scam-crisis-on-booking.com-aAj2P4i2c06f
  - Reprimanded over "only one room left" pressure messaging. https://www.brusselstimes.com/belgium/1063448/only-one-room-left-booking-com-reprimanded-for-misleading-consumers/
  - Designated an EU DMA gatekeeper (same Brussels Times source).

## Cross-cutting observations

These observations are drawn from the sources above.

- **Trust models:**
  - Marketplaces (GetYourGuide, Viator, Klook, KKday, Headout) rely on reviews from booking customers and are layering AI summaries on top.
  - Tripadvisor's own reports show fake reviews are a structural problem, at about 8% of submissions.
  - Hotel programs rely on brand curation and offer only a few hundred experiences or fewer.
  - None of these platforms uses third-party editorial or institutional citation as its trust signal. That is a possible white space.
- **Commoditisation:**
  - The same inventory is syndicated everywhere. Viator supplies Booking.com and now Airbnb, and Klook supplies Booking.com.
  - Airbnb gave up building experiences supply on its own.
- **Affiliate economics are thin:** about 4% (Viator via Awin, Booking.com attractions via CJ) up to about 8% (GetYourGuide).
- **Recurring complaints:** refunds and cancellations across all the marketplaces, and the platforms shifting blame to the operator.
- **AI distribution:** Tripadvisor/Viator are inside ChatGPT and Claude, Hilton is building a Claude connector, and GetYourGuide reportedly has a ChatGPT app. The channel for discovering experiences is moving into AI assistants. KKday explicitly blamed AI search for traffic loss.

# Experiences & local-discovery products — digital UX evidence (2023–2026)

Method: ~36 `WebSearch` queries (standard mode), October 2026. WebFetch is blocked, so every claim comes from a search-result summary and links to that result. Claims marked "unknown" were searched for and not found. Third-party teardowns (screensdesign, Landing Doctor, design-token sites) are labelled as secondary.

## Airbnb (2022 Categories → May 2025 "Stays + Services + Experiences" app)

**Visual language**
- May 2025 Summer Release shipped a new app with a "dimensional design system": new 3D iconography and a "dimensionally animated interface"; softer feel, more curved edges, built by an in-house team. — [Big Tech Is Dealing Flat Design a Death Blow (Bloomberg)](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-06-13/apple-airbnb-ditch-flat-app-icons-for-new-3d-ui-design) · [techbuzzireland](https://techbuzzireland.com/2025/05/14/redesigned-airbnb-app-now-available/)
- Icons animate on tap: the house icon opens its tiny front door (Bloomberg); the Experiences hot-air balloon "belches fire"; the Services bell shakes like a concierge bell. — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-06-13/apple-airbnb-ditch-flat-app-icons-for-new-3d-ui-design) · [Design Week — Teo Connor interview](https://www.designweek.co.uk/it-was-a-bit-nuts-teo-connor-on-designing-the-new-airbnb-app/)
- VP Design Teo Connor: "the delightful moments always have a utility behind them" — e.g. itinerary check-in/check-out shown as a lit open door vs a darkened closed door. — [Design Week](https://www.designweek.co.uk/it-was-a-bit-nuts-teo-connor-on-designing-the-new-airbnb-app/)
- 18-month project; Connor described it as feeling "both familiar and new". — [Design Week](https://www.designweek.co.uk/it-was-a-bit-nuts-teo-connor-on-designing-the-new-airbnb-app/)
- Secondhand: Airbnb is "bringing back depth, gradients, shadows, and texture"; Chesky reportedly called flat design "soulless"; depth makes interactive elements clearer. — [Dive Club newsletter "shiny grills"](https://dive-club.beehiiv.com/p/shiny-grills). This reverses the 2020 move toward flat. — [TechCrunch 2020](https://techcrunch.com/?p=1030401)
- Connor: the passport animation "has to be super fluid for it to make sense"; prototyping moved earlier; design system updated toward more 3D. — [MacStories AppStories interview](https://www.macstories.net/linked/inside-airbnbs-app-redesign-an-appstories-interview-with-marketing-and-design-leads/)
- "Lava" (secondary source only): an internal icon media format, a lightweight video with an alpha channel optimised for UI icons, built to get past the limits of Lottie and ordinary video. Not confirmed by Airbnb. — [woshipm.com](https://www.woshipm.com/ucd/6243362.html)
- Tokens (community-observed, not official): Airbnb Cereal typeface (Circular as legacy), weights 400/500/700; Rausch #FF385C (legacy #FF5A5F); the coral is used "sparingly": search CTA, active tab, primary button, wishlist heart; pill-shaped search/chips, roughly 20px card radius (other sources disagree). — [superdesign.dev](https://superdesign.dev/blog/airbnb-design-system) · [refero](https://styles.refero.design/style/c2325884-4391-4688-85cd-e143f5107517)
- Dark mode: **unknown** (searched; no source found).

**Information architecture / discovery**
- 2025: stays, services and experiences sit side by side; there is a new Explore tab for recommendations and a Trips tab that organises itineraries. — [alternativeto](https://alternativeto.net/news/2025/5/airbnb-app-redesign-adds-services-booking-trip-planning-and-upgraded-messaging) · [9to5mac](https://9to5mac.com/2025/05/13/airbnb-app-redesign-services-experiences-originals/)
- An itinerary bundles the home, booked services and experiences with reminders. — [Airbnb News 2025 Summer Release](https://news.airbnb.com/en-uk/2025-summer-release-now-you-can-airbnb-more-than-an-airbnb) · [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/computing/software/the-airbnb-app-just-got-a-massive-redesign-here-are-3-new-features-to-look-out-for)
- Scale: Services in 260 cities across 10 categories; Experiences in 650 cities. — [Hospitality Design](https://hospitalitydesign.com/news/airbnb-services-experiences-new-app/179155)
- Critique: the app "may start to feel cluttered and more confusing". — [Substack: summitstocks](https://summitstocks.substack.com/p/airbnbs-2025-summer-release)
- 2022 precedent: the homepage was rebuilt around 50+ Categories shown as a horizontally scrolling icon bar (Design, Surfing, National parks, OMG!, Amazing views…). Fast Company said it de-emphasised the search bar. — [Airbnb 2022 Summer Release](https://www.airbnb.com/2022-summer) · [Rental Scale-Up](https://www.rentalscaleup.com/video-brian-chesky-airbnb-2022-summer-release/) · [Fast Company](https://demo.fastcompanyme.com/?p=5079)

**Experiences / Originals, card and detail anatomy**
- 2025 relaunch vets hosts on expertise, connection to the city and guest feedback, with ongoing review. Originals are "designed exclusively for Airbnb", hosted by notable people (e.g. lucha libre with a pro wrestler). — [Matador Network](https://matadornetwork.com/read/airbnb-experiences-update-2025/) · [Airbnb host resources](https://www.airbnb.co.in/resources/hosting-homes/a/introducing-completely-reimagined-airbnb-experiences-742)
- Experience listings show photos in 3 places: a 4-image grid (guests, host, key detail, location), itinerary step photos (simple, plain backgrounds because they display small) and a gallery. A cover photo appears in search, with a square crop by default. — [Airbnb exp-photo-brief](https://www.airbnb.ca/e/exp-photo-brief)
- Photo rules: natural, centred, colour photos; no text overlays, staged shots or selfies. Host block has a photo, name and a "host title" saying what makes them unique. — [Airbnb experiences best practices](https://sk.airbnb.com/e/experiences-best-practices)

**Trust**
- Guest Favourites: listings in the top 1/5/10% get a golden trophy plus a "Guest favourite" badge, a highlight at the top of the listing and a label above reviews. Criteria are ratings, reviews, reliability, cancellation rate and a minimum review count. — [Airbnb help 3495](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/3495) · [Rental Scale-Up](https://www.rentalscaleup.com/airbnbs-new-highlight-system-top-1-top-5-and-bottom-10-explained/)

**Guidebooks / wishlists**
- The host guidebook is a simple welcome book of local places with an interactive map. Hosts complain about limited customisation and say it is Airbnb-only. — [Hostfully](https://www.hostfully.com/blog/airbnb-guidebook-alternatives) · [Host Tools](https://hosttools.com/blog/short-term-rental-tips/airbnb-guidebook/)
- Wishlist UX details: **unknown** (not surfaced).

**Accessibility**
- Tested with VoiceOver, TalkBack, JAWS and NVDA. Not tested: keyboard or Braille in native apps. Commits to colour-contrast minimums and reduced-motion settings. — [Airbnb help 3928](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/3928) · [Accessibility at Airbnb](https://airbnb.com/d/accessibility)

## Viator / Tripadvisor

- Viator rebrand (Duncan Channon, about Oct 2023): new logo, design system and tagline "Hello, adventure"; shapes and colours inspired by dream destinations; aimed at planning-averse travellers, leaning on last-minute booking and generous cancellation. — [Duncan Channon](https://duncanchannon.com/branding/brand-identity) · [Awin notice](https://hub.awin.com/?p=1856985)

Launched on US web first. — [Tripadvisor mediaroom](https://tripadvisor.mediaroom.com/Tripadvisor-launches-AI-powered-travel-planning-product) · [TTG Asia](https://www.ttgasia.com/2023/07/24/tripadvisors-new-feature-to-curate-personalised-itineraries-using-ai/). ## Atlas Obscura

- Voice: "wondrous, curious, and esoteric places" that guidebooks skip; anyone can add or edit. — [Boing Boing 2009](https://boingboing.net/2009/06/15/introducing-the-atla.html). About 30,000 places. — [press.atlasobscura.com](https://press.atlasobscura.com/category/news/page/3/)

- Responsive "New Look" redesign made "Add a Place" more prominent and highlights contributors (date unknown). — [Atlas Obscura](https://www.atlasobscura.com/articles/introducing-atlas-obscuras-new-look)
- iOS app: the whole database on a single map for "nearby"; custom lists for itineraries and bucket lists; a "Been There" tracker; iPhone-only, 4.8 stars. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1563250221) · [apppricinglab](https://apppricinglab.com/app/apple/1563250221)

- Keys (2024): One/Two/Three Keys for hotels ("very special" / "exceptional" / "extraordinary stay"), judged on 5 criteria including contribution to the neighbourhood. — [Wasserstrom](https://www.wasserstrom.com/blog/2024/05/07/what-are-michelin-keys/) · [The Caterer](https://thecaterer.com/all-content/guide-to-michelin-keys)

## Recurring patterns (with examples)

1. **A tiered merit badge, often top-X%, on the card and repeated on the detail page.** Airbnb Guest Favourite top 1/5/10% ([help](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/3495)); Tripadvisor Travelers' Choice 10% / Best of the Best 1% ([mediaroom](https://tripadvisor.mediaroom.com/Travellers-Choice-Best-of-the-Best-restaurants-2025)); Viator Badge of Excellence, filterable ([Viator](https://partnerresources.viator.com/?p=725)).
2. **Institutional distinction ladders with their own glyphs.** Michelin Stars/Bib/Green Star/Keys ([Michelin](https://guide.michelin.com/th/en/michelin-guide-inspectors), [Keys](https://thecaterer.com/all-content/guide-to-michelin-keys)); Infatuation 1–10 colour scale ([Mobelux](https://www.mobelux.com/projects/the-infatuation)).
3. **Own-brand "Originals" as a premium tier inside a marketplace.** Airbnb Originals ([Matador](https://matadornetwork.com/read/airbnb-experiences-update-2025/)); GetYourGuide Originals ([Landing Doctor](https://landingdoctors.com/teardowns/getyourguide-com)).
4. **Map plus numbered list, with markers keyed to list order and an explicit "not ranked" note.** Eater 38/Heatmap ([UT](https://sites.dwrl.utexas.edu/rhetoric-of-food/2017/03/29/1398/)); Atlas Obscura single map ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1563250221)); Michelin map filtered by distinction ([appfollow](https://appfollow.io/ios/the-michelin-guide/1541129177?country=be)).
5. **Horizontal category/chip rail as the first-screen navigator, sometimes demoting the search box.** Airbnb Categories ([Fast Company](https://demo.fastcompanyme.com/?p=5079)); Klook icon row ([screensdesign](https://screensdesign.com/showcase/klook-travel-hotels-leisure)).
6. **Situational ("perfect for…") filters alongside cuisine and area.** Infatuation Perfect For ([Mobelux](https://www.mobelux.com/projects/the-infatuation)); GYG duration and language filters ([screensdesign](https://screensdesign.com/showcase/getyourguide-plan-book)).
7. **Named, attributed authorship as trust.** Atlas Obscura Added By/Edited By ([page](https://atlasobscura.com/places/lake-claire-community-land-trust)); Michelin anonymous inspectors ([Michelin](https://guide.michelin.com/th/en/michelin-guide-inspectors)); Spotted by Locals vetted spotters ([iCulture](https://www.iculture.nl/gids/stedentrip-apps-iphone-ipad/)); Infatuation dated, visit-verified ratings ([Infatuation](https://www.theinfatuation.com/all/features/why-were-removing-ratings-from-the-infatuation)).
8. **Save, collect, share lists, plus a "been there" log.** Michelin shareable lists ([spark.mwm](https://spark.mwm.ai/en/apps/the-michelin-guide/1541129177)); Atlas Obscura lists + Been There ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1563250221)); GYG themed wishlists ([screensdesign](https://screensdesign.com/showcase/getyourguide-plan-book)); Klook heart on thumbnail ([screensdesign](https://screensdesign.com/explore/ui-elements/badges/)).
9. **Itinerary/Trips tab that assembles saved and booked items by day; AI generates the first draft.** Airbnb Trips ([Airbnb News](https://news.airbnb.com/en-uk/2025-summer-release-now-you-can-airbnb-more-than-an-airbnb)); Tripadvisor AI itinerary ([mediaroom](https://tripadvisor.mediaroom.com/Tripadvisor-launches-AI-powered-travel-planning-product)).
10. **Return of depth and playful, functional motion (post-flat).** Airbnb 3D animated icons ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-06-13/apple-airbnb-ditch-flat-app-icons-for-new-3d-ui-design), [Design Week](https://www.designweek.co.uk/it-was-a-bit-nuts-teo-connor-on-designing-the-new-airbnb-app/)), paired with a reduced-motion setting ([Airbnb a11y](https://airbnb.com/d/accessibility)).
11. **Authentic, unstaged photography as brand.** Airbnb bans text overlays and staged shots ([best practices](https://sk.airbnb.com/e/experiences-best-practices)); Tripadvisor 2025 builds the brand from user photos ([Creative Review](https://www.creativereview.co.uk/tripadvisor-new-rebrand-koto-studio/)).

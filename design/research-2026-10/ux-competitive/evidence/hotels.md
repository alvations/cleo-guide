# Luxury & lifestyle hotel brands — digital UX research (2023–2026 focus)

Method: WebSearch only (WebFetch blocked), 31 searches, 2026-10-10. Every claim cites the search result it came from. Many search engines returned only launch-era coverage (2017–2022); dates are flagged. Agency/brand claims are self-reported unless noted. "Unknown" = not found in search results.

**Global gaps:** Searches found no confirmed dark-mode support for any brand app ([unstar ranking context](https://unstar.app/blog/marriott-hilton-hyatt-ihg-choice-hotel-loyalty-apps-ranked-2026); dark-mode query returned nothing relevant). They also found no Awwwards Site of the Day for Aman, Belmond, Rosewood or Six Senses ([Awwwards luxury search](https://www.awwwards.com/inspiration_search/luxury/)). No measured performance data turned up for any brand.

## Marriott Bonvoy app (incl. Ritz-Carlton, W, EDITION, citizenM)

**App: redesign and visual language**
- The agency Instrument describes a "comprehensive overhaul of navigation, typography, photography, motion, color, illustration and accessibility" of the native iOS and Android apps. The work moved the app "from a booking engine to a broader travel ecosystem" with a mobile-first approach. — [Instrument: Marriott](https://www.instrument.com/work/marriott)
- Instrument made motion studies and lightweight Principle prototypes. It validated interactions in native Swift to find "performant expressions" and to fine-tune micro-interactions, so perceived performance was a stated design goal. — [Instrument: Marriott](https://www.instrument.com/work/marriott)
- An unofficial reverse-engineered design spec of marriott.com reports one typeface (Swiss 721) across 13 type tokens from 12 to 40px. Treat it with caution. — [shadcn.io Marriott Bonvoy Design System](https://www.shadcn.io/design/marriott)
- No 2024–2025 visual redesign was found. The last "refresh" coverage is from Feb 2021 (app v3.0). — [Journal des Palaces press release](https://www.journaldespalaces.com/pressrelease-59868-marriott-bonvoy-mobile-app-refreshed-to-offer-a-more-intuitive-and-personalized-experience-in-anticipation-of-increased-desire-for-travel.html); [LoyaltyLobby v3.0](https://loyaltylobby.com/2021/02/03/marriott-bonvoy-pushing-out-version-3-0-of-its-app-to-apple-users-not-available-to-android-yet/)

**App: first screen and stay-time features**
- The first screen depends on the stay. Mobile Key moves to the home screen once the guest is checked in, and the member's eligible promotions sit "front and center" on home. — [Business Traveller](https://www.businesstraveller.com/news/marriott-bonvoy-unveils-app-redesign/)
- Mobile Requests uses one-touch buttons (crib, towels, dry cleaning) plus chat with hotel staff. Non-members can now search and book. — [Hotel Management](https://www.hotelmanagement.net/tech/marriott-upgrades-bonvoy-app); [Travel Daily News](https://www.traveldailynews.com/hotels-lodging/marriott-bonvoy-mobile-app-refreshed-to-offer-a-more-intuitive-and-personalized-experience/)
- Homes & Villas and Tours & Activities can be browsed in the app, but booking hands off to a web page. — [travelingformiles](https://travelingformiles.com/whats-new-in-the-marriott-bonvoy-mobile-app/)

**App: AI search (Ask Bonvoy)**
- Ask Bonvoy is a conversational, natural-language search that went to beta in June 2026. It runs in US English on Marriott.com and the iOS/Android apps for a subset of members. It infers trip purpose and returns curated results. — [Hotel Dive](https://www.hoteldive.com/news/marriott-bonvoy-ai-search-personalization/712303/); [AltexSoft](https://www.altexsoft.com/travel-industry-news/marriott-launches-ask-bonvoy-pulls-ai-searches-into-its-app/)
- Answers are grounded only in Marriott-owned, verified property data, not the open web. This is a trust signal. A global rollout is planned for later in 2026. — [CX Dive](https://www.customerexperiencedive.com/news/marriott-bonvoy-ai-powered-search-personalize-vacation/712221/)

**App: user complaints**
- App Store users call the app "not user friendly or intuitive." A self-described UX designer complains that changing dates hides new prices behind an extra button. These are anecdotes. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/marriott-bonvoy-book-hotels/id455004730?ls=1); [JustUseApp reviews](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/455004730/marriott-bonvoy/reviews)
- A 2026 analysis of 1–3 star reviews across Marriott, Hilton, Hyatt, IHG and Choice finds the top complaints are mobile key failures, point-balance problems and award-booking errors. — [Unstar 2026 ranking](https://unstar.app/blog/marriott-hilton-hyatt-ihg-choice-hotel-loyalty-apps-ranked-2026)

**Ritz-Carlton web**
- Code and Theory redesigned ritzcarlton.com, more than 110 hotel sites and the Reserve sites, launching around Oct 2023. The redesign brought updated typography echoing the key cards, more video, a six-diamond background pattern, editorial destination guides and a mobile-friendly UI. — [Hotel Dive](https://www.hoteldive.com/news/ritz-carlton-website-refresh/690895/); [MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/388130/code-and-theory-relaunches-ritz-carltons-digital.html)
- Deloitte Digital reports about 4 minutes average reading time on "Journey" editorial pages and traffic up 12.4% year over year. These are vendor figures. — [Deloitte Digital](https://www.deloittedigital.com/us/en/work/ritz-carlton.html)
- No Ritz-Carlton-specific app was found; the brand books through Bonvoy. — [Hotel Dive](https://www.hoteldive.com/news/ritz-carlton-website-refresh/690895/)

**W Hotels web**
- Sweden Unlimited redesigned whotels.com in 2024 alongside the W rebrand. The site merges The Angle editorial magazine with hotel information, event highlights and city guides in one modular platform, with multilingual support and ADA compliance. — [Sweden Unlimited](https://swedenunlimited.com/products/w-hotels); [Shorty Awards finalist](https://shortyawards.com/17th/whotelscom-redesign)

**EDITION web**
- King & Partners designed the EDITION site at launch (2013). They built a "radically different" experience from typical hotel sites, with NOWNESS short films. The design is old and current details are unknown. — [King & Partners](https://www.kingandpartners.com/work/edition-hotels/)

**citizenM after the acquisition**
- Marriott closed the citizenM acquisition in July 2025. Properties are bookable on Marriott.com and the Bonvoy app. One report says the citizenM app is being phased out, but no official retirement date was found. — [Hospitality Net](https://www.hospitalitynet.org/news/4128283.html); [AltexSoft](https://www.altexsoft.com/travel-industry-news/marriott-welcomes-citizenm-brand-plans-more-hotels/)

## Hilton Honors app

- LoyaltyLobby (Nov 2024) calls Hilton "the most basic one of these apps, with simple graphics and functionality that contributes to the app working very well." This is low density with reliability as the value. — [LoyaltyLobby](https://loyaltylobby.com/2024/11/18/battle-of-the-hotel-chain-apps-which-one-is-superior/)
- Features from the Oct 2021 announcement:
  - Digital Key Share lets the primary guest share a key with up to 4 people, each needing an invite and login.
  - Confirmed Connecting Rooms can be booked.
  - Gold and Diamond members get upgrade notices 72 hours before arrival and choose a room in the app.
  — [Hilton Stories](https://stories.hilton.com/releases/hilton-debuts-industry-leading-tech-enhancements); [Lodging Magazine](https://lodgingmagazine.com/hilton-launches-sharable-digital-keys-and-more-tech-enhancements/)
- Since January 2026 the app shows year-to-date spend toward the new Diamond Reserve tier. This is a progress-visualization pattern and comes from a single blog source. — [The Flight Club](https://www.theflightclub.it/en/?p=110687)
- Accessibility:
  - hilton.com targets WCAG 2.1 AA and is tested with assistive technology.
  - Search results can be filtered to accessible rooms, and a dedicated view lists a hotel's accessible rooms.
  - Room descriptions cover mobility and hearing features.
  — [Hilton accessibility statement](https://www.hilton.com/en/p/accessibility/)
- An older AFB review tested the Marriott, IHG and Hilton iOS apps with VoiceOver. — [AFB AccessWorld](https://afb.org/aw/16/6/15450)
- Visual language details (type, colour) and dark mode are unknown.

- Chat is human-staffed: "There are no chatbots here." It machine-translates more than 100 languages in real time and alerts staff with visual and audio cues. Average response was 90 seconds or less (2018–19 data). — [Hospitality Net](https://www.hospitalitynet.org/news/4085206.html); [Hospitality Tech](https://hospitalitytech.com/four-seasons-chat-achieves-faster-response-times-and-expands-service)
- Chat channels are:
  - the Four Seasons app
  - Messenger, WhatsApp, WeChat, KakaoTalk and LINE
  - Apple Business Chat and SMS

  That is nine platforms as of Dec 2018. — [Hospitality News Mag](https://www.hospitalitynewsmag.com/four-seasons-chat-is-now-available-on-nine-messaging-platforms-including-whatsapp/); [Luxury Daily](https://www.luxurydaily.com/four-seasons-chat-expands-to-9-platforms//?print=1)

Typical requests are restaurant recommendations and bookings, room service, and spa or golf changes. — [Marketing Dive](https://www.marketingdive.com/news/four-seasons-live-chat-service-has-exchanged-35m-messages-since-launch/544716/); [Hotel Technology News](https://hoteltechnologynews.com/2018/12/in-expanding-its-guest-chat-services-four-seasons-takes-a-hybrid-approach-to-technology/)

A "Go Local" feature gave curated local itineraries at FS New York Downtown, the concierge-recommendation pattern. — [Luxury Travel Advisor](https://www.luxurytraveladvisor.com/hotels/four-seasons-updates-app-for-guest-convenience); [Hospitality Net](https://www.hospitalitynet.org/news/4094747.html)

## Recurring patterns

1. **The home screen changes with the stay.** A static home becomes a "next action" surface that shifts across pre-stay, check-in and in-stay: Mobile Key on home after check-in (Marriott), a contextual next-action card (citizenM), upgrade-pick notices at 72 hours (Hilton). — [Business Traveller](https://www.businesstraveller.com/news/marriott-bonvoy-unveils-app-redesign/); [Red Dot](https://www.red-dot.org/project/citizenm-55160); [Hilton Stories](https://stories.hilton.com/releases/hilton-debuts-industry-leading-tech-enhancements)
2. **Editorial and commerce merge on one surface.** Brands build magazine-style sites where reading leads to booking: Rosewood (editorial, shop and experiences), W (The Angle plus city guides), Ritz-Carlton ("Journey" pages with about 4 minutes of reading), Belmond (editorial strategy). — [Skift](https://skift.com/2025/10/08/rosewood-bets-on-discovery-and-experiences-in-brand-refresh/); [Sweden Unlimited](https://swedenunlimited.com/products/w-hotels); [Deloitte Digital](https://www.deloittedigital.com/us/en/work/ritz-carlton.html); [Matter of Form](https://creativepool.com/matterofform/projects/belmond-for-belmond)
3. **Local guides as a brand-voice asset.** Neighbourhood and city guides carry local character: Hoxton neighbourhood guides and Survival Guide, W city guides, Ritz-Carlton destination guides, citizenM city guide, Four Seasons "Go Local" itineraries. — [Hoxton](https://guests.thehoxton.com/shoreditch/survival-guide/); [Hotel Dive](https://www.hoteldive.com/news/ritz-carlton-website-refresh/690895/); [Luxury Travel Advisor](https://www.luxurytraveladvisor.com/hotels/four-seasons-updates-app-for-guest-convenience)
4. **One system with a local skin.** A shared design system lets each property carry local colour, illustration or photography: Hoxton (per-location palettes and local artists), Belmond (modular system, 60+ sites), Ritz-Carlton (110+ hotel sites). — [DEPT](https://deptagency.com/en-nl/?p=4251900); [Matter of Form](https://creativepool.com/matterofform/projects/belmond-for-belmond); [MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/388130/code-and-theory-relaunches-ritz-carltons-digital.html)
5. **Luxury means restraint.** Luxury brands use serif display type, sparse layouts, zero radius and "classical proportions" (Aman), and quiet signature colour or pattern (Rosewood Discovery Green, the Ritz-Carlton six-diamond pattern). Lifestyle brands go loud with bold type, vibrant palettes and illustration (Hoxton, citizenM). — [The Drum / Inviqa](https://www.thedrum.com/profile/inviqa/article/aman-boosts-revenue-with-new-digita-experience); [shadcn Aman](https://www.shadcn.io/design/aman); [Rosewood](https://www.rosewoodhotels.com/en/media-hub/press/2025/discovery-awaits-as-rosewood-unveils-a-new-chapter); [Creative Bloq](https://creativebloq.com/web-design/london-hoteliers-offer-quirky-online-experience-41514755)
6. **Human chat as the luxury differentiator.** Four Seasons stresses "no chatbots," human agents, 100+ languages and 90-second replies on the guest's own messaging apps. Mass brands (IHG, Hyatt, Marriott) offer chat or a digital concierge but lean toward automation and AI search. — [Hospitality Net](https://www.hospitalitynet.org/news/4085206.html); [Hospitality Net IHG](https://www.hospitalitynet.org/news/4111472.html); [Hotel Dive](https://www.hoteldive.com/news/marriott-bonvoy-ai-search-personalization/712303/)
7. **Conversational or AI search grounded in verified data.** Ask Bonvoy limits answers to verified property data, as a trust signal against hallucination. — [CX Dive](https://www.customerexperiencedive.com/news/marriott-bonvoy-ai-powered-search-personalize-vacation/712221/)
8. **Itinerary building as the core luxury app job.** Four Seasons (multi-city trip planner) and Mandarin Oriental (itinerary plus spa and restaurant booking in 17 languages) put the trip plan, not the points balance, at the centre. — [Hospitality Net](https://www.hospitalitynet.org/news/4094747.html); [Journal des Palaces](https://www.journaldespalaces.com/en/pressrelease-74401-mandarin-oriental-launches-transformed-guest-recognition-programme-and-industry-leading-guest-app.html)
9. **Showing status: progress meters vs. quiet recognition.** Mass loyalty apps show progress meters (Hilton spend toward Diamond Reserve, IHG milestones), while luxury hides the mechanics (Mandarin Oriental's invitation-only Silk/Pearl, no points). Playful brands gamify (citizenM passport stamps). — [The Flight Club](https://www.theflightclub.it/en/?p=110687); [Paris Select Book](https://www.parisselectbook.com/en/2025/05/28/mandarin-oriental-reinvents-loyalty-with-a-tailor-made-app/); [Red Dot](https://www.red-dot.org/project/citizenm-55160)
10. **Mobile-first is stated outright.** Aman, Hoxton, Mandarin Oriental and Marriott/Instrument all justify mobile-first by their mobile traffic share. — [The Drum](https://www.thedrum.com/profile/inviqa/article/aman-boosts-revenue-with-new-digita-experience); [DEPT](https://www.deptagency.com/en-au/?p=4267614); [CX Network](https://www.cxnetwork.com/omnichannel/articles/how-mandarin-oriental-digitalized-its-luxury-hotel-experience/amp); [Instrument](https://www.instrument.com/work/marriott)
11. **Reliability beats polish in user perception.** Users praise Hilton's "basic" app for working. Negative reviews across chains centre on mobile key and points failures, not aesthetics. — [LoyaltyLobby](https://loyaltylobby.com/2024/11/18/battle-of-the-hotel-chain-apps-which-one-is-superior/); [Unstar](https://unstar.app/blog/marriott-hilton-hyatt-ihg-choice-hotel-loyalty-apps-ranked-2026)
12. **Accessibility as filters and stated compliance.** Hilton offers WCAG 2.1 AA and accessible-room filters, W claims ADA compliance, and Instrument made accessibility a pillar of the overhaul. Independent audits are scarce. — [Hilton](https://www.hilton.com/en/p/accessibility/); [Shorty Awards](https://shortyawards.com/17th/whotelscom-redesign); [Instrument](https://www.instrument.com/work/marriott)

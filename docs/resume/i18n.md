## Notes

- **How it works:** pages are built in English. In the browser, `client/locale.js` swaps catalogue strings for ja.
  - Browser-drawn parts call `Cleo.t`, defined in the head snippet `l10nHead()` in `lib/render/common.js`.
  - Those parts redraw on `cleo:locale`, which fires while the page is still held; the reveal comes two frames later.
  - Data is never translated: names, write-ups, sources, collection, cuisine and area names, photos and credits.
- **Tests (`tools/beta/test-i18n.js`):** the Tokyo pseudo swap leaves 0 English interface strings, and every client `Cleo.t`/`tt` English equals en.json.
- **Gotcha:** never use `$&`, `$'` or `$$` in client JS. `test-views.js` inlines scripts with `String.replace`, where those sequences are special.
- **Rule of the catalogue:** `reviewed: true` is reserved for a human sign-off. ja stays the AI tier (`review.by: "ai"`).

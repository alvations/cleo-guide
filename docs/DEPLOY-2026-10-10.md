## Judgement calls
- `rebuild-city.py singapore --build` runs `geo-merge`, which re-stamped `verified`/`statusChecked` dates
  to today on UNVERIFIED stub entries without any re-check. That would falsely claim a check, so
  `data/geocodes.json` was reverted; the pages were rebuilt from the unchanged registry (pages don't
  depend on stub dates).
- **`beta/Singapore/toa-payoh.html` 56 → 55** is the only per-page decrease. The live *beta* mirror was
  stale: the missing record, *Guan Kee Kway Chap*, is the dataset's **Woodleigh Village** outlet (area
  PPM) and already sits on the production `Singapore/potong-pasir-macpherson.html` (live and deploy) and
  `Singapore/toa-payoh.html` was already 55 on live. The re-synced beta now carries it on
  `beta/Singapore/potong-pasir-macpherson.html` (new mirror page). The place is not cut — it moved to its
  correct page; re-adding it to the Toa Payoh mirror would make beta diverge from its source page,
  which `beta-restyle.py` asserts against. Site-wide the deploy has 5,589 more pins than live.
- **Follow-up (pre-existing, also live):** Guan Kee Kway Chap's pin in `data/geocodes.json` is the Toa
  Payoh Lor 8 stall (1.34026, 103.85442) while its record's address is Woodleigh Village Hawker Centre —
  a rule-4b misplacement for the next Singapore re-verify wave (needs a WebSearch place pin; not
  fabricated here).

# Aachen & the Dreiländereck (Euregio Maas-Rhein) — standing agent brief

A cross-border guide anchored on **Aachen**, reaching across the tri-border **Dreiländereck / Euregio
Maas-Rhein**: the German city & its StädteRegion (DE) · the Eifel & Düren (DE) · Dutch South Limburg
(NL: Maastricht, Vaals, Valkenburg) · Ostbelgien / German-speaking Belgium (BE: Eupen, Kelmis, the
Hautes Fagnes). Same pipeline + gates as every guide (docs/PIPELINE.md, docs/SOURCES.md): **discover →
fact-check (≥2 credible) → geocode + location-verify → re-rank within area → build & gate.** WebSearch
only (WebFetch blocked). Discovery agents emit **sourced research JSON with an ADDRESS but NO
coordinates** (geocoding is a later stage). This is a European, multilingual region — **search in German
AND Dutch AND French** (and Öcher Platt / Limburgs where natural), and cite native-language sources.

Target density: **as detailed as Pittsburgh** (~200+ places) — deep in Aachen itself and thorough across
the surrounding regions.

## Region & area codes (a)
- `AACHEN` — **Aachen city (DE):** the **UNESCO Cathedral (Aachener Dom / Pfalzkapelle)** — the *first*
  World Heritage Site listed in Germany (1978) — the Rathaus & Katschhof, Elisenbrunnen & the thermal
  springs (Charlemagne's spa city), the Altstadt & Münsterplatz, the **Pontviertel / Pontstraße** student
  quarter (RWTH/FH Aachen), **Burtscheid** (baroque spa quarter), Frankenberg, Lousberg, the Couven &
  Ludwig Forum & Centre Charlemagne museums, Carolus Thermen.
- `STADT` — **StädteRegion Aachen (DE):** **Monschau** (half-timbered Eifel gem, Senfmühle, Rotes Haus),
  **Kornelimünster** (abbey village, Michelin Sankt Benedikt), **Stolberg** (Burg, copper/Kupferstadt),
  Eschweiler, Würselen, Herzogenrath, Alsdorf, Baesweiler, Roetgen (Eifel gateway), Zweifall.
- `EIFEL` — **Eifel & Düren (DE):** the **Eifel National Park**, Nideggen (Burg + red-rock cliffs),
  Heimbach (smallest town, Jugendstil Kloster Mariawald / Wasserkraftwerk), the Rureifel & Rurstausee/
  Rursee, **Düren** (Leopold-Hoesch-Museum / papermaking), **Jülich** (Zitadelle, Brückenkopf-Park), Simmerath,
  the Hohes Venn edge on the German side.
- `NL` — **Dutch South Limburg (NL):** **Maastricht** (Vrijthof, Sint-Servaasbasiliek, Onze-Lieve-Vrouwe,
  Bonnefanten, Boekhandel Dominicanen, Bisschopsmolen, Sint-Pietersberg caves) plus **Vaals** (the
  **Drielandenpunt / Dreiländereck** summit + Vaalserberg), **Valkenburg** (castle ruin, marl caves,
  Christmas caves), **Gulpen/Epen** (Heuvelland), Heerlen (Roman Thermenmuseum). Food canon: **vlaai**,
  **zuurvlees**, bourgondisch dining, Limburg's Michelin bench.
- `BE` — **Ostbelgien / German-speaking Belgium (BE):** **Eupen** (capital of the DG, St-Nikolaus,
  chocolate/Jacques, the carnival), **Kelmis / La Calamine** (old Neutral Moresnet), Raeren (pottery
  castle-museum), Kettenis, the **Hautes Fagnes / Hohes Venn** high moor (Signal de Botrange, Belgium's
  roof), Bütgenbach/Büllingen lakes. Food canon: Belgian (moules-frites, frites, Belgian beer & abbey
  ales, gaufres/waffles), Ostbelgien breweries, Eupen chocolate. (Liège/Verviers may appear only as an
  edge day-trip; keep the focus on German-speaking Ostbelgien.)

## The food & drink canon — signature FIRST, then depth (name the dish)
- **Aachen / Rhineland (DE):** **Aachener Printen** (the protected gingerbread — Nobis, Klein, Lambertz,
  Van den Daele), **Reisfladen** (rice tart), Öcher **Puttes/Panhas** & Blutwurst, **Weckmann**, **Öcher

  Maastricht dining, the city's Michelin bench (**Beluga Loves You**, **Tout à Fait**, **Château

- **Ostbelgien (BE):** **moules-frites** & **frites**, **Belgian/abbey beer**, **gaufres de Liège**,
  Eupen **chocolate** (Jacques), Ostbelgien breweries, and Belgian bistro cooking.

≥2 **credible** sources per place, OR one lone institutional authority (**Michelin**, **UNESCO**,
Gault&Millau, a national monument/museum). Credible, native-language, for this region:
- **DE:** **Aachener Zeitung (AZ)** & **Aachener Nachrichten (AN)**, **WDR / Lokalzeit aus Aachen**,
  **Route Aachen / aachen-tourist.de** (official tourism), **StädteRegion Aachen**, **Eifel Tourismus**,
  **Nordeifel / Rureifel Tourismus**, **Monschau-Touristik**, Michelin Deutschland, Gault&Millau,
  Der Feinschmecker/GUSTO.
- **NL:** **Visit Maastricht / VVV Maastricht**, **Visit Zuid-Limburg**, **De Limburger** (dagblad),
  **1Limburg / L1** (regional broadcaster), **Michelin Nederland**, **Maastricht University** student guides.
- **BE (Ostbelgien):** **Grenz-Echo** (the German-language Belgian daily — the outlet of record for
  Ostbelgien), **BRF (Belgischer Rundfunk)**, **Ostbelgien.eu** (official DG tourism), **Eupen.be**,
  **Michelin Belgium**, **Visit Wallonia**.
- **Cross-border / European:** **Euregio Maas-Rhein**, **DW Travel (Deutsche Welle)**, ARTE, euronews.
- **US/international travel sources that genuinely cover THIS region (reuse the ones vetted for US cities
  where they name a specific place):** **Rick Steves** (Aachen, Maastricht), **Atlas Obscura**, **Lonely
  Planet**, **Time Out**, **Culture Trip**, **Fodor's**, **Frommer's**, **National Geographic**, **CNN
  Travel**, **Condé Nast Traveler**. Counts toward the ≥2 bar (never a lone recommender unless
  Michelin/UNESCO/Gault&Millau). Register any you use.
- **Local university & campus sources (authentic student-local):** **RWTH Aachen** & **FH Aachen**,
  **Maastricht University**, **ESN Aachen / ESN Maastricht** — student-life pages, "was tun in Aachen /
  wat te doen in Maastricht" guides, Studierendenwerk tips. Count as credible local recommendations.
- **Local bloggers & travel vloggers (viral/authentic):** verifiably-popular German/Dutch/Belgian food &
  travel bloggers, YouTubers and TikTokers who cover the region — count only with a **real, findable
  following and a specific piece naming the place**; record the creator in `CREATORS_*.json`.
- **TikTok & Reddit (younger-generation / local layer):** mine in-language hashtags and the local
  subreddits (**r/Aachen**, **r/Maastricht**, **r/Netherlands**, **r/belgium**, **r/eupen**). A **TikTok**
  counts only as a *specific findable video from a verifiably-popular account* naming the place (tag
  `"Viral"`); **Reddit** is an authentic-local **lead + corroborating vote**, never a lone-comment pin —
  pair it with ≥1 credible source and fact-check the place. See the Reddit/TikTok rules in
  [docs/SOURCES.md](../../docs/SOURCES.md).
- **Yelp / TripAdvisor / Google / OpenTable ratings count as ZERO** toward the bar (they may only
  *measure* popularity — merit bar below). Reject anonymous SEO listicles / content farms. Prefer
  **German- (and Dutch-/French-) language** sources; search in-language.

Merit bar: a mention is not merit — an award (Michelin/Gault&Millau/regional prize), a real rave, or a
high rating with real cross-platform volume before a place earns a pin. Record keep/drop in AUDIT.md.

## Output schema (mirror the other cities' files exactly)
- **Food/drink** → a JSON **LIST** file `FOOD_<scope>.json`; each record:
  `{"t":1|2|3,"a":"<area>","cz":["<cuisine strings>"],"dish":"<named signature dish>","n":"<name>",
    "address":"<full street address, Town, Country>","w":"<1-3 sentence writeup>","closed":false,
    "sources":[["KEY","https://url"],["KEY2","https://url2"]]}`
- **Sights** → a JSON **DICT** file `SIGHTS_<scope>.json`:
  `{"sources":[{"key","name","url"}...],
    "sights":[{"t":..,"a":"..","n":"..","address":"..","w":"..","k":"<kind>","g":["ICON","UNESCO","HIST",
               "ARCH","MUS","SPA","PARK","MKT","NIGHT","FREE"...],"sources":[["KEY","url"]...]}]}`
- **New outlets** → `SOURCES_<scope>.json` in the **dict** form `{"outlets":[{"key","name","type","url",
  "credible":"<why credible; note the language>"}...]}`.
- Name a **specific dish**, never a bare cuisine label. `t` = tier WITHIN the area (rank within area, not
  across the whole region). **NO lat/lng** — include the ADDRESS (geocoding is the next stage). Address
  MUST name the town + country so the map can bin it. Dedup: add NEW places only.

## Do NOT
Fabricate a place, address, dish, or source. No Yelp-only entries. No coordinates from memory. If a place
can't clear the ≥2-credible bar, leave it out (or note it in a `_dropped` list).

# Liège (Lîdje) + the Meuse valley & the Ardennes edge — standing agent brief

Fifth Belgian map, the first in **Wallonia** — a dark-engine **dataset city** under the 🇧🇪 Belgium hub
(`Belgium/index.html`), built exactly like Antwerp/Ghent/Brussels/Bruges off the shared
`tools/belgium_consolidate.py` + `tools/belgium_build.py`. Inherit everything in
[`data/belgium-research/_AGENT_BRIEF.md`](../belgium-research/_AGENT_BRIEF.md) (source bar, beer layer,
output schema, Do-NOTs). This file adds the Liège specifics.

**Key** `liege` · **page** `cities/liege.html` · **dataset** `data/liege.dataset.json` · **builder**
`tools/build-liege.py` · **density target** ~145 (≥ the Belgian peers: Antwerp 137, Brussels 139, Bruges 123).

## Areas
- **`LIE` — Liège city:** the Carré, Outremeuse (Tchantchès, the 15-août), **La Batte** Sunday market, the
  **Montagne de Bueren** (374 steps) & the Coteaux de la Citadelle, **Guillemins** station (Calatrava), the
  **Prince-Bishops' Palace**, the **Grand Curtius**, **La Boverie**, St-Paul **Cathedral** (treasury) &
  **St-Barthélemy** (Renier de Huy's baptismal font), the Cité Miroir, the Opéra royal de Wallonie, Place du
  Marché/Perron, Hors-Château, the Musée de la Vie wallonne, Aquarium-Muséum, Cointe, Sart-Tilman.
- **`LIER` — around Liège:** **Spa** (thermes, Pouhon Pierre-le-Grand, Spa-Monopole) & **Francorchamps**
  (the circuit, Stavelot abbey), **Huy** (fort, collégiale, the Bassinia), the Meuse valley (Seraing &
  **Val-Saint-Lambert** crystal, Chaudfontaine, Visé, Flémalle), the **Herve plateau** (Herve cheese,
  sirop de Liège makers, apple/pear orchards), **Val-Dieu abbey** (Aubel), **Blegny-Mine** (UNESCO
  Major Mining Sites of Wallonia), Jupille (Jupiler/AB InBev heritage), the Ardennes edge (Remouchamps
  grottes, Aywaille, Coo, Theux/Franchimont), Limbourg, the Ourthe-Amblève.

- `SPR` — Sapporo (Ōdōri · Susukino · Nijō Market · Maruyama · Moiwa · Jōzankei)
- `OTARU` — Otaru & Shakotan (canal · Sushi-ya Dōri · Yoichi Nikka · Shakotan uni)
- `NSK` — Niseko & Yōtei (Niseko · Kutchan · Rusutsu · Makkari · Kyōgoku)

- `IBURI` — Shikotsu-Tōya & Iburi (Noboribetsu Jigokudani · Lake Tōya · Upopoy · Shiraoi · Tomakomai)
- `DHOKU` — Dōhoku — Central Hokkaido (Asahikawa · Biei · Furano · Daisetsuzan · Sōunkyō)
- `TKC` — Tokachi (Obihiro · Tokachi Plain · Nakasatsunai · Ikeda)
- `DOTO` — Dōtō — Eastern Hokkaido (Kushiro · Akan · Mashū · Shiretoko · Abashiri · Nemuro)
- `SOYA` — Far North (Wakkanai · Cape Sōya · Rishiri · Rebun)

**Regioning:** Hokkaido's own **subprefectural regions** — Dō-ō (Sapporo/Otaru/Niseko/Iburi), Dō-nan (Hakodate), Dō-hoku (Asahikawa/Furano/Biei/Wakkanai), Dō-tō (Tokachi/Kushiro/Shiretoko/Abashiri) — with Sapporo as its own area.

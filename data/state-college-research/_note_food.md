## Closures verified (kept, flagged "closed": true, name carries "— CLOSED")
- Ye Olde College Diner — closed April 2018 (lease/financials); grilled-sticky tradition continues at
  the Corner Room and Berkey Creamery. [COLLEGIAN, CENTREHISTORY]
- Zola Kitchen & Wine Bar — closed Aug 2018, building demolished for The Standard development. [SCDOTCOM, COLLEGIAN]
- Herwig's Austrian Bistro — closed 2018 after 16 years (owner health/sales). [SCDOTCOM, ONWARDSTATE]
- All-American Rathskeller — closed Jan 2018; was PA's oldest continuously operating bar (est. 1933). [SCDOTCOM, ONWARDSTATE, CENTREHISTORY]
- Creekside at the Gamble Mill — closed 2025 after 4 years; Gamble Mill lodging/bar continue. [SCDOTCOM, WTAJ]

**Bình Thạnh**. This wave under-delivered against the numeric target, for two compounding reasons,
both stated rather than papered over:

1. **The shared WebSearch budget hit its hard cap (200/200) mid-wave.** WebFetch/direct fetches are
   blocked by org egress here, so WebSearch is the only channel — once exhausted I could not run the
   final second-source confirmation searches, nor the Vietnamese-language (VnExpress / Tuổi Trẻ /
   Thanh Niên) passes these districts actually need.
2. **These residential districts are genuinely thin in ≥2-credible English editorial.** Gò Vấp and
   Tân Bình results were dominated by SEO/aggregator/ratings sites (Foody, Mytour, Mia.vn, VinWonders,
   Vinpearl, Ghiền Sài Gòn, xanhsm) — none of which count toward the gate. I did **not** pad with
   those. "Popularity you can't verify isn't popularity."

Per the CLAUDE.md rules — "Gaps are stated, not filled" and never fabricate sources/creators — I kept
the JSON to what is defensible and put everything else in the **Leads** section below so a follow-up
wave (with search budget + local-language) can finish it quickly.

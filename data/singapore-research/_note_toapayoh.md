# Toa Payoh (TPY) — discovery pass note

Opening view of the Singapore + SEA guide. Discovery only (no coordinates — geocode stage follows).
Every place cleared the ≥2-credible bar (or a lone Michelin authority) and was measured before adding.

## Counts
- **FOOD: 20** — Tier 1 x5, Tier 2 x9 (incl. 2 flagged closures), Tier 3 x6.
  - Tier 1: Chey Sua Carrot Cake, Come Daily Fried Hokkien Prawn Mee, Hai Nan Xing Zhou Beef Noodle,
    Chef Kang's Noodle House, LiXin Teochew Fishball Noodles (all Michelin Bib/listed).
  - Tier 2: Kedai Makan Muhajirin, Da Ji Hainanese Chicken Rice, Ru Ji Kitchen (TPY), Old Long House Popiah,
    Lai Heng Mushroom Minced Meat Noodle, Uggli Muffins, Teochew Handmade Pau, + CLOSED: Kim Keat Hokkien
    Mee, Hup Chong Hakka Yong Tau Foo.
  - Tier 3: Hometaste Hakka Lei Cha, Pure Soya Bean, Toa Payoh Scissors Cut Curry Rice, Uncle Kun's
    Delicacies (Kun Shu), Min Kee Tanjong Rhu Wanton Noodle, Hougang 6 Miles Famous Muah Chee.
- **SIGHTS: 7** — Tier 1 x2 (Dragon Playground, Lian Shan Shuang Lin Monastery), Tier 2 x4 (Town Park &
  Look-Out Tower, Church of the Risen Christ, Block 53 'VIP Block', Toa Payoh Heritage Trail), Tier 3 x1
  (HDB Hub).

Dish canon covered: chai tow kway, Hokkien mee, Hainanese beef noodle, wanton mee, fishball noodle/mee pok,
nasi lemak, Hainanese chicken rice, popiah, bak chor mee, thunder tea rice (Hakka lei cha), tau huay,
curry rice, HK dim sum/lor mai kai, muah chee, Teochew pau, yong tau foo. Hawker centres hit: Toa Payoh
West (Lor 1), Kim Keat Palm (Lor 7), Lorong 8, Lorong 5, Toa Payoh Vista, HDB Hub Gourmet Paradise.

## Closures found (kept, flagged — notable heritage)
- **Kim Keat Hokkien Mee** — closed 28 Feb 2026 after 10 years (AsiaOne). Once top-5 Hokkien mee in SG.
- **Hup Chong Hakka Yong Tau Foo** — closed 28 Jan 2026 after four generations / 70+ years (Mothership,
  AsiaOne). Roots to a 1952 pushcart.
- Note (open, but eventful): **Teochew Handmade Pau** announced closure end-2025, then REOPENED Jun 2026
  after public demand (AsiaOne) — recorded as open.

## MEASURED & DROPPED
- **Guan Kee Fried Kway Teow** — Michelin Bib CKT, but location is Ghim Moh, not Toa Payoh (and hawkers
  retired 2023). Wrong area.
- **Lian He Ben Ji Claypot Rice** — famous, but Chinatown Complex, not Toa Payoh. Wrong area.
- **Granny's Nasi Lemak (Toa Payoh West)** — could not corroborate a 2nd credible source beyond a single
  Seth Lui list mention; Kedai Makan Muhajirin carries the nasi lemak canon instead.
- **Lai Yuan Gourmet (24h, Lor 8)** — only one credible source (Seth Lui); rest were aggregators/blogs.
- **Sims Place Chwee Kueh (Kim Keat)** — only a personal blog found; no 2nd credible source. Dropped
  rather than pad the chwee-kueh slot.

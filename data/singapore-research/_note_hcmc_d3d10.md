## Why only 5, and the honest constraint

**The shared WebSearch budget hit 200/200 before I could lock a SECOND credible source for a
large batch of otherwise-strong finds.** The guide's rule is strict — ≥2 credible outlets, or a
lone Michelin; Saigoneer/Coracle/Vietcetera each count as ONE, and Yelp/TripAdvisor/Foody/Google
= zero. Rather than pad the array with single-credible places (violating the bar) or fabricate a
second source, I shipped only what clears the bar and parked the rest below. This follows
"gaps are stated, not filled." A short follow-up wave (one credible 2nd source each — ideally a
Vietnamese daily: VnExpress / Tuổi Trẻ / Thanh Niên / Znews) converts most of these quickly.

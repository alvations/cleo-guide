## Notable finds
- **Cháo Lòng Bà Út (D1)** — the direct answer-in-kind to the seed: an 80-year gánh cooked in a pot
  welded from two aluminium basins, Bà Út alone selling 60+ years. (Tuổi Trẻ + VietnamNet.)
- **Kem Vi Bổn (D1)** — 1958 tube ice cream, founder learned the craft in Cambodia; 4 generations on a
  near-70-year recipe. A rare heritage DESSERT/kem institution.
- **Cơm Tấm Thuận Kiều (D11)** — the *oldest cơm tấm brand in Saigon*, born on Thuận Kiều St before 1975
  (Người Lao Động long-read + VietKings).
- **Bún Bò Dồi Chiên Cô Minh (Phú Nhuận)** — genuinely unique dish (fried dồi in the bún bò bowl),
  unchanged since 1984; two top-tier dedicated features.
- **Mì Vịt Tiềm Quảng Huê Viên (Phú Nhuận)** — one of Saigon's oldest Cantonese kitchens, owners trace
  it to a 1940 congee shop; distinct from the existing D5/D6 Hoa mì houses (Huê Ký, Hải Ký, Thiệu Ký).

## Notable finds

- **Chùa An Phú / "Miểng Sành" (D8)** — a ~180-year pagoda skinned in 30+ tonnes of broken porcelain
  shards (Vietnam Book of Records). The single strongest new sight and a genuine District 8 signature.
- **Đình Bình Đông (D8)** — a 150-year communal house alone on a 2-ha islet mid–Kênh Đôi, a national
  relic reached by ferry; the on-the-water heritage that makes D8 worth a canal tour.
- **Bến Bình Đông Tết flower market (D8)** — the "trên bến dưới thuyền" sampan flower market on Kênh
  Tàu Hủ, the city's most atmospheric Lunar-New-Year scene (2026 edition sourced).
- **Hủ Tiếu Hồ Đỗ Khiêm (D8)** and **Bún Chua Cay Macao (D6)** — two near-vanished Hoa/Teochew noodle
  dishes at their reference addresses, each with two national-outlet features.

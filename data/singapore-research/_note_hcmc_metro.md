## Notable finds
- **Suoi Tien** — world's first Buddhism-themed park; Wikipedia + Atlas Obscura, now on Metro Line 1.
- **Buu Long Tourist Area (Bien Hoa)** — the "Ha Long Bay of the South"; DISTINCT from the D9 Buu Long
  Pagoda (Gotama Cetiya) already in the guide.
- **Dai Nam Wonderland** — Vietnam's largest tourist complex (711 ha) incl. the giant Kim Dien temple.
- **Vam Sat (Can Gio)** — Ramsar bird/bat sanctuary; a separate ticketed site from the already-listed
  Monkey Island in the same UNESCO reserve.
- **Cu Lao Pho** — the South's 18th-c. trading port, now a cluster of national-relic Chinese temples.

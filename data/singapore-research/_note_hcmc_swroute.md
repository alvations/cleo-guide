## Notable / viral finds
- **Hủ Tiếu Mỹ Tho** — the corridor's most famous dish. Kept the two best of the **five houses province-certified** 'Quán hủ tiếu Mỹ Tho uy tín, chất lượng 2024' (Báo Ấp Bắc + Tuổi Trẻ + SGGP): **Hủ Tiếu 44** (pork) and **Hủ Tiếu Cá Thảo** (fish). Not padding — this is an official shortlist.
- **Kẹo dừa Bến Tre (Thanh Long)** — THE coconut-candy of the coconut land; founder Nguyễn Thị Vinh's face on the wrapper is a provincial icon (Báo Đồng Khởi + VnExpress).
- **Bánh tét lá cẩm nhà họ Huỳnh** — the Bình Thủy family that *invented* Cần Thơ's purple sticky-rice log (bà Sáu Trọng); named among Vietnam's Top-100 dishes (Znews + Báo Cần Thơ + Tuổi Trẻ). Strongest-sourced food item in the wave.
- **Nhà cổ Huỳnh Thủy Lê** — the real house behind Marguerite Duras' Goncourt-winning 'The Lover' / L'Amant (Wikipedia + Thanh Niên).
- **Làng gạch gốm Mang Thít** — Vĩnh Long's 'Kingdom of Red Kilns', ~800 beehive kilns being conserved as contemporary heritage (VnExpress + PLO + vietnamtourism.gov).
- **Cồn Sơn** — genuine community tourism with the delta's famous 'flying snakehead fish' (Dân trí 2025 + Cần Thơ tourism); a fresher pick than the standard floating-market loop.
- **Cầu Cần Thơ** — on opening (2010) the longest cable-stayed main span in SE Asia (Wikipedia).

## Honest constraint — why 8, not 20–30
The shared **WebSearch budget hit 200/200** mid-wave (same wall the D3D10 and north waves hit), and this
central-north slice is heavily mined already (264 existing names). The guide's gate is strict — **≥2
credible outlets, or a lone Michelin/Wikipedia**; Saigoneer/Coracle/Vietcetera each count as ONE, and
Foody/TripAdvisor/Google/Grab/Mia/listicles = zero. Most strong D10/D11 street-food candidates have exactly
ONE credible source (a single Saigoneer Hẻm Gems or a single Vietnam Coracle feature) plus only aggregators.
Rather than pad the array with single-credible places or fabricate a second source, I shipped only what
clears the bar and parked the rest below ("gaps are stated, not filled"). A follow-up wave with fresh
WebSearch budget converts most parked items with ONE Vietnamese-daily query each.

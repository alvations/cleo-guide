- **Nhau Nhau** — Peter Cuong Franklin's local-canon cocktail bar above MICHELIN-starred Anan; on Asia's 50 Best Discovery.
- **Pasteur Street Brewing** — the alley taproom where Saigon craft beer began (2015); the single most important craft-beer address in the city.
- **Chill Skybar** and **Saigon Saigon Bar (Caravelle, since 1959)** — the city's defining rooftop landmarks, absent from the guide.
- **NOB** — Vietnam's first natural-wine bar; a genuine "first," not padding.
- **Lacaph** — colonial-shophouse coffee-experience space + roastery telling Vietnam's coffee story (a strong Vietnamese-coffee institution the guide lacked).

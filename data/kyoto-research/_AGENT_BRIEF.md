## Areas (use these exact ids in every record's `a`)
- `HGS` — Higashiyama-ku (Gion · Kiyomizu-dera · Sannenzaka · Kennin-ji · Yasaka · Tōfuku-ji)
- `SAKYO` — Sakyō-ku (Ginkaku-ji · Philosopher's Path · Nanzen-ji · Okazaki · Demachiyanagi · Shimogamo)
- `CTR` — Central — Nakagyō & Shimogyō (Nishiki · Pontochō · Kawaramachi · Karasuma · Nijō Castle · Kyoto Station)
- `KITA` — Kita & Kamigyō (Kinkaku-ji · Daitoku-ji · Imperial Palace · Nishijin · Kamigamo)
- `RKSAI` — Rakusai — Ukyō & Nishikyō (Arashiyama · Sagano · Ryōan-ji · Katsura · Koke-dera)
- `FSHMI` — Fushimi & Minami (Fushimi Inari · Tō-ji · Fushimi sake district · Daigo-ji)
- `RKHKU` — Rakuhoku mountains (Ōhara · Kurama · Kibune · Takao · Miyama)
- `UJI` — Uji & Nara (Byōdō-in · Uji tea · Tōdai-ji · Kasuga Taisha · Nara Park)
- `KYFU` — Kyoto Prefecture & Lake Biwa (Amanohashidate · Ine no Funaya · Kameoka · Ōtsu · Hiei-zan)

**Regioning:** Kyoto city's 11 **wards (-ku)** grouped as locals do (Rakuchū centre, Rakutō east, Rakusai west, Rakuhoku north, Rakunan south), plus Uji/Nara and the wider prefecture as the day-trip ring.

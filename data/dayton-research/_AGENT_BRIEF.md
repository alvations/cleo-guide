# Dayton — standing brief for every research agent

Region: **Dayton OH + the Miami Valley** (Kettering, Oakwood, Centerville, Beavercreek, Fairborn, Huber
Heights, Vandalia, plus Yellow Springs as a day trip). Birthplace of aviation. 6 areas.

## The bar
Notable / authentic / hidden-gem / popular / viral / iconic — and above all **CREDIBLE**, with the
specific claim/dish named. Density is a yardstick, never a quota — never pad.

## MULTIPLE SOURCES OF TRUTH — hard, gated rule
Every place needs **≥2 independent CREDIBLE sources**, OR a single **institutional authority** (James
Beard, or the **NPS** for the federally-run aviation-heritage sites). *Dayton has no Michelin/Eater* — the
local press is the backbone. Yelp/TripAdvisor = **open-verification only, ZERO**. `node
tools/research.js --sourcecheck dayton-oh` must PASS before build.

## The marquee + food
- **Sights first (Dayton's real draw is aviation):** the **National Museum of the U.S. Air Force**
  (world's largest military aviation museum, free — the marquee), **Carillon Historical Park** (the
  Wright Flyer III), **Dayton Aviation Heritage NHP** (Wright Cycle Company, Wright-Dunbar, Huffman
  Prairie, Hawthorn Hill), Dayton Art Institute, SunWatch Indian Village, Boonshoft, RiverScape
  MetroPark, the **Oregon District**, Woodland Cemetery, the Dayton Arcade; **Yellow Springs** (Glen
  Helen, Clifton Gorge, Young's Jersey Dairy, John Bryan SP).
- **Food (local classics):** **Marion's Piazza** (Dayton-style pizza — thin, square/party-cut), **Esther
  Price** candy, **Bill's Donuts** (Centerville), **the Pine Club** (steakhouse), the Oregon District
  bars (Century Bar, Trolley Stop, Lucky's), El Meson (West Carrollton), Salar & Lily's (Oregon
  District), Coco's Bistro, Corner Kitchen, plus breweries (Warped Wing, Carillon Brewing — museum
  brews to 1850s recipes, Toxic, Branch & Bone) and Young's Jersey Dairy (Yellow Springs).

# Cincinnati — standing brief for every research agent

Region: **Cincinnati OH proper + the Northern Kentucky riverfront** (Covington, Newport, Bellevue, Ft.
Thomas) that faces downtown across the Ohio River. 7 areas — see area ids below.

## The bar (every place must clear it)
Notable / authentic / hidden-gem / popular / viral / iconic — and above all **CREDIBLE**, with the
specific claim/dish named. **Density is a yardstick, never a quota — never pad.** Prune or hold anything
not credibly sourceable.

## MULTIPLE SOURCES OF TRUTH — hard, gated rule
Every place needs **≥2 independent CREDIBLE sources**, OR a single **institutional authority** — which
in this market means **James Beard** (nominee/semifinalist/winner). *Cincinnati is outside the Michelin
Guide's US coverage and has no Eater edition*, so the credible backbone is the local press below.
Yelp/TripAdvisor/OpenTable are **open-verification only and count as ZERO**. `node tools/research.js
--sourcecheck cincinnati-oh` must PASS before build.

## Source palette — exhaust these BEFORE Yelp
1. **Institutional authority:** JAMESBEARD (awards/semifinalists — the top mark here).
2. **Local of record:** ENQUIRER (Cincinnati Enquirer / cincinnati.com — the daily; strong food desk),
   CINCYMAG (Cincinnati Magazine — city mag criticism + Best Of), CITYBEAT (alt-weekly + "Best Of
   Cincinnati" reader poll).
3. **Reported TV / lifestyle:** WCPO, WLWT, WKRC, FOX19; CINCYREFINED (Cincinnati Refined), SOAPBOX
   (Soapbox Cincinnati), MOVERSMAKERS.
4. **Sights / curated:** OFFICIAL, WIKIPEDIA (published coords), ATLASOBSCURA, NPS (Taft NHS / Freedom
   Center context), OHIOHISTORY, VISITCINCY (official CVB — good for hours, not a lone food recommender),
   USATODAY (10Best polls, e.g. the perennial Cincinnati-chili rankings).
5. **Open-check only (never the recommender):** YELP / TRIPADVISOR / OPENTABLE.

Source KEYS: JAMESBEARD, ENQUIRER, CINCYMAG, CITYBEAT, WCPO, WLWT, WKRC, FOX19, CINCYREFINED, SOAPBOX,
MOVERSMAKERS, OFFICIAL, WIKIPEDIA, ATLASOBSCURA, NPS, OHIOHISTORY, VISITCINCY, USATODAY, YELP (open-check).

## Food — start with what Cincinnati is FAMOUS for (the opening move)
Name the canon first, then find the iconic/hidden-gem places serving it: **Cincinnati chili** (Skyline,
Gold Star, Camp Washington Chili, Empress, Dixie, Pleasant Ridge Chili — 3-way/4-way/5-way & coneys) ·
**goetta** (Glier's; diner plates) · **Montgomery Inn** ribs · **Findlay Market** (oldest continuous
public market in Ohio — Eckerlin Meats, Pho Lang Thang) · **Graeter's** (French-pot black-raspberry
chip) · **BonBonerie / Busken** (opera cream, schnecken) · **LaRosa's** pizza · **German/OTR** heritage
(Mecklenburg Gardens) · the **craft-beer revival** (Rhinegeist, MadTree, Braxton NKY) · modern
Cincinnati fine dining (Sotto, Boca, Salazar, Jose's, Pho Lang Thang). Then NKY (Covington/Newport).

## Always
- Fact-check OPEN (2025/2026) from a real source; closures kept-but-flagged (`— CLOSED`) or excluded (log why).
- Name a specific dish; tag the **kitchen's own tradition**, never one shared dish.
- FULL street address required. **NEVER invent coordinates.**
- Grade tiers within area & cuisine. Reply with counts + every exclusion + reason.

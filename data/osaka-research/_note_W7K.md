I filed bunka.nii.ac.jp (文化遺産オンライン) under BUNKACHO; see SOURCES_OSAKA_W7K.json. A kunishitei.bunka.go.jp/…/102/2212 result also surfaced, but I couldn't confirm which item it is, so I didn't cite it.

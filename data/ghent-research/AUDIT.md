## Source bar applied
Every entry carries ≥2 credible sources OR one lone institutional authority (Michelin / Gault&Millau /
UNESCO / a national/city museum / official regional tourism). Native sources used: **Visit Gent**,
**Stad Gent**, **VisitFlanders**, **VRT NWS**, **Michelin België**, **Gault&Millau België**, **Culinaire
Ambiance**, **Onroerend Erfgoed** (Flemish heritage inventory), **UNESCO Vlaanderen**, **Toerisme

Industriemuseum, Museum Dhondt-Dhaenens, mudel, Gruut, De Hopduvel, Souvenir, Vrijmoed), plus press/
editorial (Knack Weekend, Feeling, ELLE BE, Marie Claire BE, National Geographic NL, Atlas Obscura,
time to momo, June, Le Fooding, Rutger Bakt). **Yelp / TripAdvisor / Google / RateBeer / Untappd were
used as ZERO toward the bar** (only ever to cross-check that a place exists/measures popularity).
`gentdekuip.com`, `hipsteadresjes.gent`, `reizen-en-reistips.nl`, `bevegan.be` are treated as
corroborating (paired with an official/press source), never as a lone recommender.

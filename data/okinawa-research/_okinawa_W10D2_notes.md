- **Kiraku:** the RS 933592 attribution comes from a search summary of a multi-result page. The co-trip 613167 page was not line-verified to name Kiraku.
- **Sakurazaka Brewery:** OCVB 4398 matches on content. Feel Japan 29346 surfaced again in the KANEHIDE pin search, so it may cover KANEHIDE rather than Sakurazaka. This is the weakest pairing of the batch.
- **Amuro:** Feel Japan's list item may refer to the Shuri Kubagawa shop. The record uses the Rurubu Shuri Taira shop at 1-66.
- **Senbero-ya:** nahanavi.com is a private local guide, NOT the Naha City Tourist Association (naha-navi.or.jp = NAHANAVI). It has its own new key, NAHANAVICOM.
- **RYUKYURYORI:** this is a certification scheme, not a merit award, so it is treated as one credible source. It is never used alone.

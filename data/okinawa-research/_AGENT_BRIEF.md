# Okinawa (沖縄) — standing agent brief

Read `data/japan-research/_AGENT_BRIEF.md` first (shared Japan rules, source bar, output schema). This file
adds only what is specific to Okinawa.

## Areas (use these exact ids in every record's `a`)
- `NAHA` — Naha (Kokusai-dōri · Makishi Public Market · Tsuboya · Shuri Castle · Naminoue · Sakaemachi)
- `CHUBU` — Chūbu — Central Okinawa (Chatan & American Village · Okinawa City/Koza · Ginowan · Urasoe · Yomitan)
- `NANBU` — Nanbu — Southern Okinawa (Itoman · Nanjō & Sēfa-utaki · Peace Memorial Park · Tomigusuku)
- `HOKBU` — Hokubu — Northern Okinawa (Nago · Motobu & Churaumi · Kouri Island · Onna · Yanbaru)
- `KRM` — Kerama & nearby islands (Tokashiki · Zamami · Aka · Kume-jima · Iheya)
- `MYK` — Miyako Islands (Miyako-jima · Irabu · Ikema · Kurima)
- `YAEYA` — Yaeyama Islands (Ishigaki · Iriomote · Taketomi · Hateruma · Yonaguni)

**Regioning:** Okinawa Prefecture's own regional division: the main island's **Hokubu / Chūbu / Nanbu** (north/central/south) with **Naha** as its own area, then the outlying island groups — **Kerama**, **Miyako** and **Yaeyama**.

## Food canon — the opening move (name it, then find who serves it best)
Okinawa soba & soki soba (and Yaeyama/Miyako soba), gōyā champurū, rafute, agu pork, taco rice (King Tacos, Kin), jūshī, umibudo, inamuruchi, sata andagi, Blue Seal ice cream, zenzai, awamori distilleries, Orion beer, A&W Okinawa, steak houses (post-war Koza culture), Makishi market 'mochiage' upstairs cooking, Ishigaki beef.

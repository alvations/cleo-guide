# Akron · Kent · Canton — standing brief for every research agent

Region: **Summit, Portage and Stark counties** (+ the Wadsworth edge of Medina Co.) — Akron and its
neighborhoods, the north-Summit suburbs **up toward the Cuyahoga County line** (Cuyahoga Falls, Stow,
Hudson, Peninsula, Bath, Richfield, Twinsburg, Macedonia), Kent & Portage (Kent State, Ravenna, Aurora,
Streetsboro, Garrettsville), Barberton/Norton/Wadsworth/Green, Canton & North Canton (+ Hartville,
Louisville, Alliance) and Massillon & southern Stark (Canal Fulton, Navarre, Brewster, Wilmot).

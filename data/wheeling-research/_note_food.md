## Signature canon covered
- **DiCarlo's Pizza** (WHL) — the original Wheeling-style cold-cheese square pizza (Wheeling shop since 1949).
- **Coleman's Fish Market** (WHL) — Centre Market fish sandwich since 1914.
- **WV pepperoni rolls** — Greco's GrecoRoni (WHL).
- Valley Italian institutions — Figaretti's (WHL, 2026 James Beard America's Classics), Angelo's (WASH, est. 1939),
  Undo's West (OHV), Union Grill (WASH), Adornetto's (ZAN, est. 1958), Old Market House Inn (ZAN, closed).
- Vagabond Kitchen (WHL, chef Matt Welsch), Later Alligator (WHL crepes), Ye Olde Alpha / Alpha Tavern (WHL).
- **Tom's Ice Cream Bowl** (ZAN) — Tin Roof Sundae since 1948. Weasel Boy Brewing (ZAN).
- National Road anchors — Century Inn (Scenery Hill PA, 1794, oldest inn on the National Road), Theo's coney
  dogs (Cambridge, 1931), Mehlman's Cafeteria (St. Clairsville, 1961), Kennedy's Bakery (Cambridge, 1925),
  Gulla's Lunch (Bellaire coney), Sarris Candies (Canonsburg), Shorty's Lunch (Washington PA hot dogs).

## Notable award / institutional authority
- **Figaretti's Restaurant (Wheeling)** — 2026 James Beard Foundation **America's Classics** award (Southeast).
  Verified: James Beard Foundation, WV Public Broadcasting, WTOV9, CBS Pittsburgh, Restaurant Business, Visit Wheeling.
  Only the second WV restaurant ever so honored (after Jim's Steak & Spaghetti House, 2019).
- Adornetto's — Pizza Today national Top-100 independent pizzerias.
- Mehlman's — WTRF / Ohio.org "best cafeteria in Ohio."
- Century Inn — National Register of Historic Places (Hill's Tavern), oldest continuously operating inn on the National Road.

## Closures (fact-checked, kept-flagged closed:true)
- **Old Market House Inn (Zanesville)** — closed May 31, 2025 after 51 years; the space is reopening as
  Market Street Prime (Times Recorder / WHIZ News). Kept flagged.
- **Wheeling Brewing Company (Centre Market)** — permanently closed; a notable 2014-era downtown-revival
  brewpub (Weelunk/Lede News/WV Tourism). Kept flagged.
- **Ye Olde Alpha (Wheeling)** — the ~90-year original closed early 2020 and the old building was demolished;
  NOT flagged closed as a dead end because it was rebuilt and revived on-site as **The Alpha Tavern** (open),
  which is the record used (named honestly as "formerly Ye Olde Alpha").

## Access / open-closed notes (verified 2026)
- **WV Independence Hall** — OPEN, free tours Tue-Sat 9-5 (closed Sun/Mon).
- **Wheeling Suspension Bridge** — standing/OPEN; historically carries traffic, periodic restrictions/repairs.
- **Centre Market** — OPEN; shops Mon-Sat, closed Sun. **Oglebay** — OPEN year-round; Festival of Lights is
  the Nov-Jan seasonal draw.
- **Monument Place (Shepherd Hall)** — standing, NRHP; owned by Osiris Shrine Temple, **interior access
  limited to events/appointment**, not a walk-in museum. Flagged in the blurb (not closed).
- **Meadowcroft Rockshelter** — OPEN **seasonally (~May-Oct)**; Heinz History Center site.
- **Belmont County Victorian Mansion Museum** — OPEN **year-round by appointment**.
- **Mosser Glass** — factory tours **Mon-Fri only** (morning/early-afternoon windows); showroom Mon-Fri.
- **National Museum of Cambridge Glass** — **seasonal Apr-Oct** (Wed-Sun), limited Nov-Dec hours.
- **Dickens Victorian Village** — **seasonal Nov 1-Jan 1** only.
- **The Wilds** — **seasonal**: daily May-Sept, weekends Oct; **advance reservations required**; safaris ticketed.
- **National Road & Zane Grey Museum / John & Annie Glenn Museum** — OPEN (Ohio History Connection; check
  seasonal days); ticketed. **Zanesville Museum of Art** — OPEN Wed-Sat. **Alan Cottrill Studio** — OPEN
  Mon-Sat, suggested donation. **Lorena Sternwheeler** — seasonal cruises.
- **Salt Fork / Blue Rock / Dysart Woods** — OPEN, public parks/preserves (Dysart now held by Captina
  Conservancy after Ohio University transfer, 2022; preserved and open).

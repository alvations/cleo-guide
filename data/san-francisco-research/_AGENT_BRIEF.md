# San Francisco & Peninsula — standing brief for every research agent

Region: **San Francisco proper + the northern Peninsula down to San Mateo, incl. the SFO corridor**
(Daly City, Brisbane, South San Francisco, San Bruno, Millbrae, Burlingame, Hillsborough, San Mateo,
Foster City) — this is the northern bridge to the Silicon Valley guide, which starts around Menlo
Park/Redwood City. Coast: Pacifica. **Do not re-cover** places south of San Mateo (that's the SV guide).

## The bar (every place must clear it)
Notable / authentic / hidden-gem / popular / viral / iconic — and above all **CREDIBLE**, with the
specific claim/dish named. **Density is a yardstick, never a quota — never pad.** Prune or hold anything
not credibly sourceable.

## MULTIPLE SOURCES OF TRUTH — hard, gated rule
Every place needs **≥2 independent CREDIBLE sources**, OR a single **institutional authority**
(Michelin star/Bib, James Beard). Yelp/TripAdvisor/OpenTable are **open-verification only and count as
ZERO**. `node tools/research.js --sourcecheck san-francisco-ca` must PASS before build.

## Source palette — exhaust these BEFORE Yelp
1. **Guides of record:** MICHELIN (star/Bib/Green), JAMESBEARD (nominee/semifinalist/winner).
2. **Elite food media:** INFATUATION (SF), KQED / Bay Area Bites. *(EATERSF, SFCHRON, THRILLIST are
   credible but crawler-BLOCKED — cite only if a title/quote surfaces; don't burn searches on the domains.)*
3. **SF local of record:** SFSTANDARD (SF Standard), HOODLINE, MISSIONLOCAL (Mission Local), SEVENXSEVEN
   (7x7), BOLDITALIC (The Bold Italic), TIMEOUT (Time Out SF), SFGATE.
4. **Reported TV:** NBCBAY, ABC7, KRON4, KTVU.
5. **Sights/curated:** OFFICIAL, WIKIPEDIA (published coords), ATLASOBSCURA, NPS (GGNRA/Alcatraz/Presidio),
   CASTATEPARKS, SFPARKS (SF Rec & Park), SFTRAVEL (visitor bureau).
6. **Vetted creators:** a real-following, findable SF food/travel creator.
7. **Open-check only (never the recommender):** YELP / TRIPADVISOR / OPENTABLE.

## Food — start with what SF is FAMOUS for (the opening move)
Name the SF canon first, then find the hidden-gem/iconic places serving it: **Mission burrito & the
taqueria belt · cioppino & Dungeness crab · sourdough (Boudin/Tartine/Josey Baker) · Cantonese &
dim sum (Chinatown, Richmond, Millbrae) · Tenderloin Vietnamese (Little Saigon) · the Bay Area's
famous Burmese (tea-leaf salad) · North Beach Italian · oysters (Hog Island, Swan) · Irish coffee
(Buena Vista) · third-wave coffee (Blue Bottle/Ritual/Sightglass) · It's-It · Golden Gate fortune
cookie factory · Cal-cuisine fine dining (Michelin)**. Then the Peninsula/SFO corridor: Millbrae &
South SF dim sum, San Mateo, Burlingame, Daly City Filipino.

## Always
- Fact-check OPEN (2025/2026) from a real source; closures kept-but-flagged (`— CLOSED`) or excluded (log why).
- Name a specific dish; tag the **kitchen's own tradition**, never one shared dish.
- FULL street address required. **NEVER invent coordinates.**
- Grade tiers within area & cuisine. Reply with counts + every exclusion + reason.

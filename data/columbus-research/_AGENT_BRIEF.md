# Columbus — standing brief for every research agent

Region: **Columbus OH** (Ohio's capital) and its neighborhoods + inner suburbs. 7 areas.

## The bar
Notable / authentic / hidden-gem / popular / viral / iconic — and above all **CREDIBLE**, with the
specific claim/dish named. Density is a yardstick, never a quota — never pad; prune or hold what isn't
credibly sourceable.

## MULTIPLE SOURCES OF TRUTH — hard, gated rule
Every place needs **≥2 independent CREDIBLE sources**, OR a single **institutional authority** (James
Beard — *Columbus has no Michelin Guide or Eater edition*, so JB is the top mark and the local press is
the backbone). Yelp/TripAdvisor/OpenTable = **open-verification only, count as ZERO**. `node
tools/research.js --sourcecheck columbus-oh` must PASS before build.

## Food — start with what Columbus is famous for
**Jeni's Splendid Ice Cream** (born here) · the **North Market** (Jeni's, Hot Chicken Takeover, Brezel) ·
**Columbus-style pizza** (thin, square/party-cut, edge-to-edge pepperoni — Massey's, Rubino's, TAT
Ristorante, Iacono's, Harvest) · **Schmidt's Sausage Haus** (German Village — Bahama Mama, cream puffs) ·
**Thurman Cafe** (the Thurmanator burger) · **Dirty Frank's** hot dogs · **buckeye** candy
(Anthony-Thomas) · one of the **largest Somali/East-African** communities in the US (Hoyo's, Banadir,
Hamdi) + Nepali/Bhutanese (Northland) · fine dining (The Refectory, Veritas, Wolf's Ridge, Lindey's,
Speck, Agni). Then breweries (Land-Grant, Seventh Son, BrewDog USA) and the Short North / German Village
scenes.

## Always
Fact-check OPEN (2025/2026); closures kept-but-flagged (`— CLOSED`) or excluded (log why). Name a dish;
tag the kitchen's own tradition. FULL street address. NEVER invent coordinates. Grade tiers within
area+cuisine. Reply with counts + exclusions.

# Columbus SIGHTS expansion — wave 2 (SIGHTS_EXPAND2.json)

15 NEW, non-duplicate sights (checked against `columbus.dataset.json` P[47] and `SIGHTS_EXPAND.json`).
Every place ≥2 credible sources OR a lone institutional authority (Metro Parks / official museum).
Yelp/TripAdvisor never counted. NO coordinates written (geocode pass handles per rule 4a).

## By area
- **DTN (5):** Ohio Theatre *(t1, National Historic Landmark movie palace, CAPA+Wikipedia)*; Southern
  Theatre *(t2, oldest surviving theatre in central Ohio, 1896)*; Columbus Commons *(t2, free downtown
  park+carousel)*; Union Station Arch @ McFerson Commons *(t3, salvaged 1897 Burnham arch)*; Columbus
  Metropolitan Library — Main Library *(t3, award-winning 2016 renovation, free)*.
- **OSU (1):** Studio 35 Cinema & Drafthouse *(t2, Clintonville; Columbus's oldest cinema / last
  independent; first US theater with a liquor license)*.
- **EAST (1):** Schumacher Gallery, Capital University, Bexley *(t2, free; 3,000+ works incl.
  Rembrandt/Picasso/Warhol)*.
- **WEST (1):** Columbus Idea Foundry, Franklinton *(t2, world's physically largest makerspace, free
  tours)*.
- **BURB (7):** Early Television Museum (Hilliard) · Anti-Saloon League Museum (Westerville) · Sharon
  Woods Metro Park (Westerville) · Blendon Woods Metro Park + Walden Waterfowl Refuge (Westerville) ·
  Slate Run Living Historical Farm (Canal Winchester, 1880s working farm) · Hayden Run Falls (Dublin,
  35-ft waterfall — distinct from the already-listed Indian Run Falls) · Easton Town Center
  (retail-tourism, 30M visitors/yr). All t2.

Tiers: 1×t1, 12×t2, 2×t3. DTN gains a new tier-1 (Ohio Theatre); other areas retain existing tier-1s.

## OPEN / access notes (fact-checked 2026)
All 15 open. Access caveats recorded in each record's `k`:
- Schumacher Gallery — free but **academic-year only** (closed summers/university holidays).
- Early Television Museum — free, **weekends only** (Sat 10–6, Sun 12–5).
- Columbus Idea Foundry — free evening tours by RSVP; classes/membership ticketed.
- Slate Run Farm — free, seasonal hours.
- Ohio Theatre — free guided tours on select summer Saturdays; otherwise a ticketed venue.
No closures found among the new set.

## MEASURED & DROPPED / not added
- **American Whistle Corporation** (only US metal-whistle maker, north Columbus) — genuine unique claim,
  but sourcing was official + travel-tour aggregators only (no top-tier local editorial or museum/park
  authority), and tours are **reservation-only (min-party)**. Failed the ≥2-credible bar → dropped.
- **Palace Theatre** (CAPA, 1926) — would stack a 3rd near-identical downtown CAPA theatre; kept Ohio
  (landmark) + Southern (oldest) as the two distinct picks → dropped as padding.
- **Whetstone Park** (Clintonville) — the already-listed Park of Roses sits inside it → overlap, skipped.
- **Santa Maria replica ship** (former Scioto Mile attraction) — dismantled/removed; nothing to visit,
  so not added.

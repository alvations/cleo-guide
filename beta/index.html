Silicon Valley

Not one city but the South Bay — Palo Alto, Mountain View, Sunnyvale, Cupertino, Santa Clara and San Jose. Apple Park, the Computer History Museum, Stanford and the Winchester Mystery House; and the Valley's real signature, its immigrant kitchens — arguably the best Taiwanese, Vietnamese and South Indian eating in America, plus the boba it invented. Wheeling & the National Road

The Ohio Valley and the old National Road (I-70) — Wheeling, WV (the Suspension Bridge, Oglebay, Centre Market, Independence Hall) and nearby Washington, PA, then west through St. Clairsville, Cambridge (Dickens Village, Salt Fork) and Zanesville (the Y-Bridge, the pottery legacy, The Wilds) to where the Columbus guide begins. Valley food: DiCarlo's pizza, Coleman's fish sandwich, pepperoni rolls, Tom's Ice Cream Bowl, and James-Beard-honored Figaretti's. Sourced to the Wheeling Intelligencer, Weelunk, the Observer-Reporter and the Zanesville Times Recorder; each place merit-measured with a source-cited coordinate.
